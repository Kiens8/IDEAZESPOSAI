# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: October 16, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

IDEAZES POS AI ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our services.

This policy applies to all users of IDEAZES POS AI services, including restaurant owners, staff, and customers whose data may be processed through our platform.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide

| Category | Examples | Purpose |
|----------|----------|---------|
| Account Information | Name, email, phone, business name | Account creation and management |
| Business Information | Restaurant name, address, tax ID | Compliance and invoicing |
| Payment Information | Credit card details, bank account | Payment processing |
| Staff Information | Names, roles, contact details | Staff management |
| Customer Information | Names, phone, email, preferences | Loyalty and order management |

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically

| Category | Examples | Purpose |
|----------|----------|---------|
| Usage Data | Pages visited, features used, timestamps | Service improvement |
| Device Information | Browser type, OS, IP address | Security and analytics |
| Transaction Data | Order details, payment amounts | Service provision |
| Performance Data | API response times, errors | Troubleshooting |

### 2.3 Information from Third Parties

- Payment processors (Stripe, ABA Payway)
- Integration partners
- Public business registries

## 3. How We Use Your Information

### 3.1 Primary Uses

We use your information to:

- **Provide Services**: Process orders, manage inventory, handle payments
- **Account Management**: Create and maintain your account
- **Communication**: Send service notifications, support responses
- **Security**: Detect and prevent fraud, unauthorized access
- **Compliance**: Meet legal and regulatory obligations

### 3.2 Marketing Uses

- We do NOT sell your business data
- We may send product updates and promotions (you can opt out)
- Marketing emails include unsubscribe options

### 3.3 Aggregate Data

- We may use aggregated, anonymized data for analytics
- This data cannot identify you or your business

## 4. Data Sharing

### 4.1 We Do NOT

- Sell your personal or business data
- Share customer data with other businesses
- Use your data for targeted advertising

### 4.2 We MAY Share Data With

| Recipient | Purpose | Data Shared |
|-----------|---------|-------------|
| **Payment Providers** | Process payments | Transaction data |
| **Delivery Partners** | Order delivery | Order details, customer contact |
| **Legal Authorities** | Legal compliance | When required by law |
| **Service Providers** | Infrastructure, support | Minimal necessary data |

### 4.3 International Transfers

- Data may be processed in Malaysia and other countries
- All transfers comply with applicable data protection laws
- Adequate safeguards are in place for international transfers

## 5. Data Retention

### 5.1 Retention Periods

| Data Type | Retention Period |
|-----------|----------------|
| Account data | Duration of subscription + 90 days |
| Transaction records | 7 years (tax compliance) |
| Customer data | Until account deletion + 90 days |
| Logs | 12 months |
| Backups | 30 days |

### 5.2 Data Deletion

- You may request deletion of your data
- Some data may be retained for legal compliance
- Deleted data is permanently overwritten within 90 days

## 6. Data Security

### 6.1 Technical Measures

- **Encryption**: 256-bit SSL/TLS for data in transit
- **At Rest**: AES-256 encryption for sensitive data
- **Access Control**: Role-based access control (RBAC)
- **Authentication**: Two-factor authentication (2FA) support
- **Monitoring**: 24/7 security monitoring and alerts

### 6.2 Organizational Measures

- Regular security audits and penetration testing
- Employee background checks and training
- Access on a need-to-know basis
- Incident response procedures

### 6.3 Physical Security

- Cloud infrastructure in secure data centers
- ISO 27001 certified facilities
- 24/7 physical security

## 7. Your Rights

### 7.1 Access Rights

You may request:
- A copy of your personal data
- Details of how we use your data
- Categories of data processed

### 7.2 Correction Rights

You may request correction of:
- Inaccurate personal data

### 7.3 Deletion Rights

You may request deletion of:
- Personal data no longer necessary

### 7.5 Objection Rights

- Processing based on legitimate interests
- Direct marketing

Response time: Within 30 days
