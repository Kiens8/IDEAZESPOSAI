# Payment methods

Your counter only offers the payment methods you have switched on for the branch. You switch them on under **Settings**, on the **Payment** tab. The tab needs the Payment feature on your plan, and **Settings** itself is admin-only.

## The methods you can turn on

**Accepted Payment Methods** holds eleven tick boxes:

| Method | What it is |
|---|---|
| **💵 Cash** | Physical money taken at the counter |
| **💳 Credit/Debit Card** | A card machine at the counter |
| **DuitNow QR** | Malaysian QR payment |
| **🇰🇭 KHQR (Cambodia)** | Cambodian QR payment |
| **🇸🇬 SGQR (Singapore)** | Singaporean QR payment |
| **🇻🇳 VietQR (Vietnam)** | Vietnamese QR payment |
| **Touch 'n Go** | Malaysian e-wallet |
| **GrabPay** | E-wallet |
| **Boost** | E-wallet |
| **ShopeePay** | E-wallet |
| **🏦 Bank Transfer** | A transfer to your account |

The counter screen names the same methods in the customer's own words: **Cash**, **Card (Credit/Debit)**, **DuitNow QR**, **KHQR (Cambodia)**, **SGQR (Singapore)**, **VietQR (Vietnam)**, **Touch 'n Go**, **GrabPay**, **Boost**, **ShopeePay**, **Bank Transfer** and **E-Wallet**.

If the branch has no method list saved, the counter falls back to **Cash**, **Card (Credit/Debit)** and **DuitNow QR**.

<Note>
Switching a method on does not make an online payment work. Ticking **DuitNow QR** gives you the button on the counter; to take the money through it you also need a payment account connected to the branch. See below.
</Note>

## Split bill, tips and cash change

Three more switches on the same tab change how the counter behaves:

| Setting | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Split Bill** | Lets you split one bill between several people | On |
| **Tips Collection** | Turns on tips | Off |
| **Cash Change Calculation** | Shows the change due when cash is taken | On |

With **Split Bill** on, you split the bill equally or by item, and each person pays their own share. The method you choose for each person starts at **Cash**.

With **Cash Change Calculation** on, the counter shows **Change due** after you enter the cash received. An under-tender never shows a negative change.

<Note>
**Tips Collection** turns tips on, but the counter screen has no tip control yet. Turning it on does not give you a way to add a tip while you take the money.
</Note>

## Taking the money

You record the payment from the order. The Orders screen offers **💰 Record Payment**, and the counter screen (Staff Order) opens the same payment panel. Both show the same method names for the same tender.

Only **DuitNow QR** and **Touch 'n Go** can be taken through the online gateway. Every other method is simply recorded against the order.

## Online QR payment

Online QR payment needs two things at once:

1. The branch is in **Malaysia**.
2. The branch has a **connected payment account**.

If either is missing, the counter cannot take an online QR payment.

<Note>
A branch outside Malaysia cannot take an online QR payment at all. The **Online payments** section on the **Payment** tab says **Paying online is only available for outlets in Malaysia - DuitNow and Touch 'n Go are Malaysian payment methods.** and shows no form.
</Note>

A branch in Malaysia that has no connected account still sees the form, but the online payment mode stays locked on the **Order** tab with the message **Pay online when the customer orders - not available yet - the restaurant has no payment account connected**.

### Connecting a payment account

The **Online payments** section of the **Payment** tab holds the connection. The app talks to **HitPay**.

| Field | What you put in it |
|---|---|
| **Provider** | **HitPay** |
| **API key** | Your HitPay API key |
| **API secret (salt)** | Your HitPay API secret |
| **Webhook salt** | The salt of your HitPay webhook. Optional — leave it empty to use the API secret |
| **Merchant ID** | Your merchant id, for example `H12345` |
| **Currency** | **MYR** |

Both **API key** and **API secret (salt)** are required. If one is missing you get **The API key and the API secret are both required.** When the save works, the notice **The payment account was saved.** appears and the two secret boxes are cleared from the screen.

The **Webhook salt** field explains that the secret is stored on the server and never shown again, and that HitPay signs its events with the salt shown next to your webhook URL.

### What you can read back

Once the account is connected, the section shows **Connected** along with the **Provider**, **Currency**, **Merchant ID**, the **API key** (only its last few characters, behind an ellipsis) and **Last updated**.

The credentials are write-only. You can replace them with new ones, but you cannot read the current ones back.

### Disconnecting

The disconnect button asks **Disconnect the payment account? The guests will not be able to pay online until a new account is saved.** When you confirm, the notice **The payment account was disconnected.** appears.

## When the money is taken

The payment mode per order type is set on the **Settings** screen, on the **Order** tab, under **When the money is taken**. Each order type you serve — **Dine-in**, **Takeaway**, **Delivery** — gets one of:

- **Pay when the customer collects**
- **Pay at the counter first (we start after the payment)**
- **Pay online when the customer orders**

An order takes a snapshot of the choice when it is created, so changing it later does not move the orders already running.
