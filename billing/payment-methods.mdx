# Payment Methods

IDEAZES POS AI supports multiple payment options for your customers.

## Accepted Payment Methods

| Method | Description | Processing Time |
|--------|-------------|-----------------|
| Cash | Physical currency | Instant |
| Credit Card | Visa, Mastercard, AMEX | Instant |
| Debit Card | Bank cards | Instant |
| Online Banking | FPX, bank transfer | 1-2 days |
| QR Pay | GrabPay, Touch 'n Go, Boost | Instant |

## Setting Up Payment Methods

### Step 1: Access Settings

1. Go to **Settings** → **Payment**

### Step 2: Enable Methods

Toggle on the payment methods you want to accept:

- Cash
- Credit/Debit Card
- Online Banking
- QR Payment

### Step 3: Configure (Optional)

For card payments, connect your Stripe account:

1. Enter Stripe API keys
2. Test the connection
3. Enable live mode when ready

## Processing Payments

### Counter Service

1. Complete the order
2. Select payment method
3. Process payment
4. Print receipt (if configured)

### Table Service

1. Update order status to "Served"
2. Select payment method
3. Process payment
4. Print receipt

## Pay Later Option

For subscription payments:

1. Select Pay Later at checkout
2. Receive invoice via email
3. Pay via bank transfer within 14 days

## Payment Reports

View payment analytics:

- Daily sales by payment method
- Transaction history
- Settlement reports
