# Subscription Billing

**Last Updated:** September 24, 2026

---

## How Subscription Billing Works

When you subscribe to a IDEAZES POS plan, you are billed according to your selected billing cycle. Here's what you need to know:

### Billing Cycles

| Billing Cycle | Description |
|---------------|-------------|
| **Monthly** | Billed every month on your subscription start date |
| **Annual** | Billed once per year (save up to 20%) |

### How Trials Work

When you start a free trial:

| Day | What Happens |
|-----|--------------|
| Day 0 | Trial starts, first invoice created ($0) |
| During Trial | You have full access to all features |
| Trial Ends | Your first paid invoice is generated |
| After Payment | Your subscription continues |

### Upgrading Your Plan

You can upgrade your plan at any time. Here's how it works:

#### Trial Users
- You can upgrade to a higher plan during your trial
- Your billing cycle will be set when you upgrade
- **No payment is required during the trial period**
- When your trial ends, your first paid invoice will be generated

#### Paid Users
- Upgrading to a higher plan is immediate
- You may be charged a prorated amount for the remaining days in your current billing period
- Your billing cycle can be changed (monthly ↔ annual)

### Payment Methods

We offer two payment methods:

| Method | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Credit/Debit Card (Stripe)** | Secure payment processed by Stripe |
| **Pay Later** | Manual payment via bank transfer |

#### Important Notes

- **If you select Stripe as your payment method**, you must continue using Stripe for all future upgrades
- **If you select Pay Later**, you can switch to Stripe during upgrades
- Once your subscription is active, you cannot switch to Pay Later if you originally chose Stripe

### Invoice Timeline

| Event | Invoice Amount | Status |
|-------|---------------|--------|
| Trial starts | $0 | Paid (auto) |
| Trial ends | Full period price | Pending |
| First payment | Full period price | Pending |
| Subsequent renewals | Full period price | Pending |

### Renewal Process

1. **7 days before** - First reminder email sent
2. **3 days before** - Second reminder email sent  
3. **1 day before** - Final reminder email sent
4. **Due date** - Invoice marked as overdue
5. **Grace period** - Access continues (14 days)
6. **After grace period** - Subscription suspended

### Cancellation

- You can cancel at any time
- Access continues until the end of your current billing period
- No refunds for unused time

### Reactivation

- You can reactivate your subscription at any time
- Your POS data is preserved and will be available immediately after reactivation
- A new subscription is created (previous subscription remains on record)
