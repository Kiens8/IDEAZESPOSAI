## Plan Upgrades

### How Upgrades Work

When you upgrade your plan, the following rules apply:

1. **Billing Cycle Preservation**: Your existing billing cycle (monthly or annual) is preserved during upgrades. If you're on an annual plan, you upgrade to annual pricing of the new tier.

2. **Prorated Charges**: For upgrades, you'll pay the prorated difference based on the remaining days in your current billing period.

3. **Changing Billing Cycle**: To switch from monthly to annual (or vice versa), wait for your current subscription to expire, then resubscribe with your preferred billing cycle.

### Example Upgrade Scenarios

| Current Plan | Billing | Upgrade To | Result |
|-------------|---------|------------|--------|
| Basic | Annual | Pro Annual | Annual pricing preserved |
| Basic | Monthly | Pro Monthly | Monthly pricing preserved |
| Basic | Annual | Pro Monthly | Stays Annual (not changed to monthly) |
| Basic | Monthly | Pro Annual | Stays Monthly (not changed to annual) |

### Why Preserve Billing Cycle?

- **Predictable Budgeting**: Annual customers planned for yearly payments
- **Fairness**: No unexpected billing cycle switches mid-subscription
- **Simplicity**: Clear, straightforward upgrade experience

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I change plans?

Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade at any time. During upgrades, your existing billing cycle is preserved.

### Is there a free trial?

Yes, Starter plan is free forever. No credit card required.

### What payment methods do you accept?

We accept:
- Credit/Debit cards (Visa, Mastercard)
- Bank transfer (Pay Later option for annual billing)

### How does billing work?

- **Monthly Billing**: Pay each month at the start of your billing period
- **Annual Billing**: Pay once per year at a discounted rate (save ~17%)
- Billing cycle is preserved during upgrades

### Can I switch from monthly to annual?

Yes! To change your billing cycle:
1. Wait for your current subscription period to end
2. Resubscribe with your preferred billing cycle (monthly or annual)

This ensures clean, transparent billing without complex proration calculations.
