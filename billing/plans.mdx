## Plan Details

### Starter - Free

Perfect for small food stalls or testing:

- Up to 100 orders/month
- 1 outlet
- 2 staff accounts
- Basic features

### Basic - $10/month

Ideal for small restaurants:

- 1,000 orders/month
- 1 outlet
- 5 staff accounts
- Full POS features
- Basic reports

### Pro - $20/month

For growing restaurants:

- Unlimited orders
- 5 outlets
- 20 staff accounts
- Full POS + Reports
- Priority support

### Enterprise - $50/month

For franchises and large operations:

- Unlimited everything
- Custom branding
- API access
- Dedicated support

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I change plans?

Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade at any time.

Yes, Starter plan is free forever. No credit card required.

### What payment methods do you accept?

We accept:
- Credit/Debit cards (Visa, Mastercard)
- Bank transfer (Pay Later option)

### How does billing work?

Billing is monthly or annually. Annual billing includes a discount.
