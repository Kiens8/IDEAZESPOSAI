# Customer app features

## Order history

The app keeps the orders of the table the guest is at, and shows them under **My Orders**.

Two things scope the history:

- The **branch and table** it belongs to.
- The **dining customer** it belongs to. The history belongs to one guest, not to whoever picks up the phone next.

A guest who signs in sees their own orders. On a shared device, the next guest does not inherit them.

## Cancelling an order

The venue panel — the bar showing the restaurant and the table — opens a sheet with the table's active orders. **Cancel my order** there cancels the table's active orders. The venue panel is also where the guest picks a different table, which cancels the table's orders first.

## Account and profile

The account section holds the guest's details, and the sign-in state is remembered on the device.

When the guest is signed in, the app also shows the **loyalty** and **voucher** sections. When the guest is not signed in, the app falls back to the restaurant's default settings for which features are shown.

## Loyalty points

When the restaurant runs a loyalty programme, the guest sees their points and earns more on each order.

## Vouchers

When the restaurant runs vouchers, the guest can apply a voucher to the order and see the discount on the cart. A welcome voucher can greet a guest who signs up for the first time.

## AI order assistant

The app carries an AI assistant that helps build the order. It opens as a chat panel and can:

- **Search the menu** — ask for "something spicy" or "vegetarian" and it suggests the matching dishes.
- **Add items to the cart** — the suggestions are clickable, and picking one adds it to the cart.

The same search sits on the menu, so the guest can look up a dish without opening the assistant.

## Availability

The menu shows stock honestly. Items that are out of stock are marked and cannot be ordered, and low-stock items ask for confirmation first. The stock state comes from the branch, so it reflects what the kitchen can actually serve.

## Offline behaviour

The app keeps a cached copy of the shell on the device, so it opens without a connection and shows a banner: **You're offline - the menu may be out of date**. The menu itself is fetched fresh from the branch each time the app opens with a connection, so prices and availability stay correct. Placing an order needs a connection.
