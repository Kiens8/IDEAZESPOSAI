# Install the customer order app

The customer order app is a progressive web app. Once it is installed, it opens from the home screen with its own icon and runs full screen, without the browser address bar.

Installing is optional. A guest can order straight from the browser at **order.ideazes.com**.

## Before you start

You need the link to the app for your restaurant. There are two ways to get it:

- **Scan the table QR code.** The app opens with the branch and table already known.
- **Open the app yourself.** The app then asks you to pick the restaurant, the branch, and the table.

## iPhone and iPad

iOS does not show an install button. You add the app to the home screen by hand.

1. Open the app in **Safari**. Other browsers on iOS cannot add an app to the home screen.
2. Tap the **Share** button — the square with an arrow pointing up.
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Confirm the name and tap **Add**.

A few seconds after the app opens, it also shows a short written banner with the same steps, so you can follow it on screen.

The app then appears on your home screen under the restaurant's name and icon.

<Note>
If you do not see **Add to Home Screen**, confirm you are in Safari. Chrome on iOS can save a page to the home screen, but it does not install the app.
</Note>

## Android

When the browser supports installation, the app shows an **Install app** button that opens the browser's own install prompt.

1. Open the app in **Chrome** or another Chromium browser.
2. Tap the **Install app** button in the app.
3. Confirm **Install**.

<Note>
The **Install app** button appears only when the browser allows installation. On other browsers you install from the browser's own menu — share, then **Add to Home screen**.
</Note>

## What you get after installing

- **Full screen** — no browser bars, no address bar.
- **The right venue on open.** The app remembers the restaurant, branch, and table you chose, so it reopens there even when you launch it from the home screen with no QR code. It does not ask you to pick the venue again.
- **The restaurant's name and icon** on the home screen. The name and the icon are applied once the app knows the venue, so a first-time guest sees a generic name and icon until they pick a branch.
- **A cached copy of the app** on the device, so it still opens when the connection is down.

## Offline behaviour

When the app loses the connection it shows a banner that says **You're offline - the menu may be out of date**. Browsing continues with what was last loaded, but the menu itself is fetched fresh from the branch each time the app opens with a connection, so prices and availability stay correct.

You cannot place an order while offline.
