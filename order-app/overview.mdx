# Customer App - Customer Ordering

The customer ordering app at **order.ideazes.com** allows customers to browse menus and place orders online.

## Features

### For Customers
- **QR Code Ordering** - Scan table QR code to view menu and order
- **Venue Picker** - Choose the restaurant and the table (group -> branch -> table); the picker works offline and the choice is remembered
- **Branded App** - The app on the home screen carries the name of the group, the icon and the colour of the branch
- **Menu Browsing** - View categories, items, and modifications
- **Cart Management** - Add/remove items, modify orders
- **Order Tracking** - Real-time order status updates
- **Pay at the counter** - the guest orders from the table, the staff records the payment on the POS. The guest app does not take money

### Order Flow
1. Customer scans QR code or visits order.ideazes.com (the picker asks for the restaurant and the table when the venue is unknown)
2. Selects items and customizations
3. Submits order
4. Order sent to the POS (the kitchen display shows it)
5. The staff serves the order and **records the payment on the POS** - the guest pays at the counter
6. Customer receives status updates

## Key Concepts

### Table Management
- Each table has a unique QR code
- QR codes link to specific outlet and table
- Customers can order without waitstaff
- The table stays occupied while an order is open - the payment is settled at the counter

### Menu Integration
- Menu synced from POS backend
- Real-time menu updates
- Item availability based on outlet settings
