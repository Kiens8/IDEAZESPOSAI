# Customer order app

The customer order app is the web app your guests open to order from the table. It runs at **order.ideazes.com** and installs to the home screen like any other app, so it opens full screen without the browser bars.

The app is branded for the restaurant. The app name comes from the restaurant group that owns the branch, and the icon and the theme colour come from the branch. Both are applied once the guest's venue is known, so a guest who has not picked a branch yet sees a generic name and icon until they do.

## What the guest gets

- **Install to the home screen** on iOS and Android — see [Install the app](install).
- **A venue picker** — restaurant, then branch, then table — when the app opens without a table QR code.
- **Dine in and takeaway** ordering, each switched on per branch. A branch that also delivers shows a delivery chip on the order type row.
- **The menu** with categories, options, and add-ons.
- **A cart** that survives closing the app.
- **Payment** at the counter, or online by QR when the branch offers it.
- **Order tracking** with live status.
- **Order history** for the table, tied to the dining customer.
- **Loyalty points and vouchers** when the restaurant offers them.
- **An AI order assistant** that searches the menu and helps build the order.

## The two apps are different

| | Customer order app | Delivery app |
|---|---|---|
| Address | `order.ideazes.com` | `order.ideazes.com/d/<branch>` |
| Where the guest is | At a table in the branch | At home |
| Order types | Dine in, takeaway, and delivery where the branch enables it | Delivery |
| Requires an account | No | Yes |

Delivery to the door is a separate app with its own storefront, its own menu, its own delivery fee, and a driver on the way. See the [delivery app guide](../delivery-app/overview).

The delivery chip in the in-venue app is a per-branch setting. A branch that has delivery switched off never shows it, so a table guest only sees a delivery option if that branch really delivers.
