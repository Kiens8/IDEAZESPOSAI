# Reports

The **Reports & Analytics** screen tells you how the business is doing. You pick a period, you choose the report you want, and the screen gives you the numbers, a few plain-language insights, a chart, and a table you can take away as a CSV file or print.

## Who can see reports

Open it from the sidebar with **Reports**. The screen is for **manager and admin accounts** — a staff account does not see it. Your plan also has to include the Reports feature, otherwise the screen does not appear in the menu.

Some reports need an extra entitlement before they appear at all:

- The **Delivery** tab only appears if your restaurant has delivery analytics switched on.
- Every other report appears with the Reports feature itself.

<Note>
If you cannot find the **Delivery** tab, it is not a permission problem. The tab is hidden unless the delivery analytics entitlement is present.
</Note>

## Choose the period

The date range sits at the top right of the screen. You choose between:

- **Today**
- **Last 7 Days**
- **Last 30 Days**
- **All Time**

Everything on the screen — the cards, the insights, the chart and the table — reflects the range you picked.

One tab is the exception. **Daily Takings** keeps its own date field, labelled **The day of the takings**, and the range dropdown is disabled while that tab is open.

## Every report compares against the previous period

Each number card can carry a comparison line. The app works out the previous window as a window of the same length immediately before the one you selected, so **Last 7 Days** is compared with the seven days before it.

The comparison shows an arrow and a percentage, with the words **vs the previous period**:

- **↑** the value went up.
- **↓** the value went down.
- **→** the value did not change.

**All Time** has no earlier period, so its cards carry no comparison line.

## The tabs

### Sales

What the period earned, and at what margin. The cards are **Total Revenue**, **Total Orders**, **Avg Order Value**, **Items Sold**, **Tax Collected**, **Discounts Given**, **Gross Profit** and **Gross Margin**. A bar chart of revenue by period sits above the table, and the table below it is the same series the chart draws, so the export adds up to the chart.

**Gross Profit** and **Gross Margin** show **—** if your restaurant has never valued its dishes. They need a valued menu before they can say anything.

### Orders

How the period's orders split by type. The cards are **Total Orders**, **Paid**, **Pending** and **Cancelled**. The **Order Breakdown** table lists **🍽️ Dine In**, **🥡 Takeaway** and **🚚 Delivery** with the count, the revenue and the share of the total.

### Walk-in vs Registered

Whether your business is building a customer base or living on walk-ins. The cards are **Registered**, **Walk-in**, **Registered Sales** and **Walk-in Sales**. The **Customer Type Analysis** table splits the period between **👤 Registered Customers** and **🚶 Walk-in Customers**.

An insight box reads this back to you. If members dominate, it says **Good news! Registered customers account for `{{percentage}}`% of orders.** If walk-ins dominate, it suggests **Consider promoting loyalty sign-ups — `{{percentage}}`% of orders are from walk-ins.**

### Items

The full menu mix for the period — every dish sold, not a top ten. The cards are **Total Items Sold**, **Top Item**, **Items Revenue**, **Avg Item Price**, **Gross Profit** and **Gross Margin**.

The **Top Selling Items** table gives you each item's quantity sold, revenue, cost and contribution. A second table, **Breakdown by size**, breaks the same sales down by size.

<Note>
The size table is not the whole story. Items sold without a size are excluded from it, and the note under the table says so: **`{{quantity}}` items were sold without a size and are in the table above only.** The cost shown also depends on how your costs are recorded — the report tells you which rule it used, from a recipe valued at current ingredient prices, a frozen cost at the moment of sale, an estimate, or a mix of rules.
</Note>

### Payments

Where the money came from. The cards are **Total Revenue**, **Cash**, **Card** and **Pending Payment**, and the **Payment Methods** table gives transactions, amount and share of the total for each method. A payment that is still pending is shown separately, not as revenue.

### Loyalty

Your customer base over the period. The cards are **Total Customers**, **Active Customers**, **Total Revenue** and **Total Points Redeemed**. The **Customer Loyalty Overview** table lists every customer with their orders, revenue, redeemed points and lifetime points.

### Daily Takings

The closing sheet for one day. The cards are **The Takings**, **The Orders**, **The Average Ticket** and **The Voided**, and the **The takings of the day** table is a statement of lines: what was taken, what was voided, and a note on each line.

This tab has its own date field and loads its own numbers for the day you pick, plus the day before it for the comparison.

### Delivery

The delivery trade on its own. The cards are **Delivery Revenue**, **Fee per Order**, **Delivery Orders** and **Avg Order Value**, and the **Delivery by outlet** table breaks the numbers down per outlet with orders, paid, cancelled, revenue and fee per order.

### Audit

Two halves of the same question. The cards are **Voided Orders**, **Total Voided**, **Reprints** and **Copies Printed**.

The first table, **Voided and refunded orders**, shows which order was voided, when, why, by whom and for how much. The second table, **The reprint log**, shows every reprinted receipt, how many copies, on which printer and why.

## The insight panels

Some tabs carry an extra panel under **💡 Insights**, written in plain language:

- **Today so far** (on the Sales tab, and only when the range is **Today**) compares today's takings so far with the same time yesterday. It shows the pace of the day.
- **When we serve** (on the Sales tab) shows the busiest hour and how the day's orders split across **Breakfast**, **Lunch**, **Dinner** and **Other**. This is the input you need for rostering.
- **Top customers** (on the Loyalty tab) lists your five best customers of the period. The table below it holds the whole list.

## Download a CSV

Every tab has a table, so every tab can be exported with **📥 Download CSV**. The file is written for an accountant rather than for a spreadsheet:

- It opens with three header lines naming the **Outlet**, the **Report** and the **Period** it covers, plus **Generated at** — the moment the file was generated — and a **Summary** block of the key numbers.
- Money cells are bare numbers, with no currency symbol and no thousands separator, so they can be summed.
- Percentages carry two decimals.
- Khmer names survive Excel on Windows.
- A guest name starting with `=` is escaped so it cannot run as a formula in the spreadsheet.

The filename is `report_<id>_<from>_<to>.csv`.

If the table has no rows, the button reads **No data to export** rather than writing an empty file.

The Audit tab's two tables each have their own export, and the second table's file is a separate report.

## Print a report

Every tab can also be printed with **🖨️ Print**. This uses your browser's print window, and the printed page carries a header with the outlet, the period, when it was printed and by whom.
