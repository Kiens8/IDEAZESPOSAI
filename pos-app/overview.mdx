# IDEAZES POS app user guide

The IDEAZES POS app is the screen your staff work from during service. This guide walks through everything in it: taking an order at the counter, running the floor, building your menu, and reading the numbers at the end of the day.

Every page here describes what the app actually does today. Where the app behaves in a way that will surprise you, the page says so plainly instead of hiding it.

## Where to start

If you are new, read these three pages first.

- [Take an order at the counter](taking-orders) — the screen your cashier lives in during service.
- [Manage live orders](order-management) — the screen your floor manager watches.
- [Settings](settings) — the switches that decide how the rest of the app behaves.

## How the app decides what you can see

Two things decide which parts of the app you get. Both matter, and they are not the same thing.

**Your role** decides what you can reach.

| Role | What you reach |
|---|---|
| **Staff** | Dashboard, Orders, Delivery, Staff Order, Kitchen, Order History, Tables and Staff |
| **Manager** | Everything Staff reaches, plus Menu, AI Chatbot, AI Tools, Reports and the seven Inventory screens |
| **Admin** | Everything Manager reaches, plus Outlets, Promotions, Loyalty, Vouchers and Settings |

**Your plan** decides which features are switched on. The sidebar only shows a screen when your plan includes that feature. So a screen can be missing for two different reasons: you do not have the role, or your plan does not include it.

<Note>
Settings is admin-only. A manager and a staff member never see it in the menu.
</Note>
