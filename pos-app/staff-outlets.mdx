# Staff and branches

This page covers the two screens that decide who works in your restaurant and where they work: **Staff Management** and **Outlet Management**.

Open them from the sidebar with **Staff** and **Outlets**. **Staff** is for every staff member. **Outlets** is admin-only. Each screen needs its own feature on your plan, so one that your plan does not carry does not appear in the menu.

## Signing in

You sign in with two things:

- **Email**
- **Password**

Type them and press **Sign In**. The screen also has a **Show password** eye toggle if you want to check what you typed.

There is no branch picker on the sign-in screen. Your branch comes from your staff record, and your role comes from the same record. If your account has been blocked, the screen shows **Access Blocked** and you are not signed in. If your account carries warnings, they appear under **Important Notice** and you still sign in.

<Note>
The **PIN** on a staff record is not a way to sign in. There is no PIN field on the sign-in screen, so a staff member who forgets their password cannot use their PIN to get in.
</Note>

To sign out, use the **Log Out** button at the bottom of the menu (or the red **Log Out** tile on a phone).

## The three roles

Every staff member has one of three roles. The role decides what they can reach.

| Role | What the role description says | What it reaches |
|---|---|---|
| **Admin** | Full access: All settings, staff, outlets, menu & orders | Everything, including **Outlets**, **Promotions**, **Loyalty**, **Vouchers** and **Settings** |
| **Manager** | Manage: Menu, orders, tables & staff clock in/out | Everything a staff member reaches, plus **Menu**, **AI Chatbot**, **AI Tools**, **Reports** and the inventory screens |
| **Staff** | View: Orders & tables. Edit: Own profile (name, email, password, PIN) | **Dashboard**, **Orders**, **Delivery**, **Staff Order**, **Kitchen**, **Order History**, **Tables** and **Staff** |

A staff member never sees **Reports**, the menu editor or **Settings**. Those entries are not in their menu at all.

The **Staff** screen itself is in everyone's menu, but what you can do on it depends on your role.

## Staff Management

The screen is headed **Staff Management** with the subtitle **Manage staff accounts, roles & clock in/out**. Each row shows the person, their **Outlet**, their **Role**, their **Status** (**Clocked In** or **Clocked Out**), and the action buttons.

The header also shows how many staff records you have used against your plan, as **`{{used}}` / `{{max}}` staff used**. When you reach the limit, the app refuses with **Maximum staff limit reached (`{{max}}`). Upgrade your plan to add more staff.** and points you to support.

### Adding a staff member

Press **Add Staff** to open the **Add New Staff** form. It asks for:

| Field | What you put in it |
|---|---|
| **Name** | The person's name. Required. |
| **Email** | The address they will sign in with. Required. |
| **Password** | Their sign-in password. Required when you create them. |
| **PIN (optional)** | Up to six digits, used for clock in and clock out. |
| **Interface Language** | The language this person sees in the POS. |
| **Role** | **Staff**, **Manager** or **Admin**. Admins only. |
| **Assign to Outlet** | The branch they belong to. Admins and managers only. |

The field under **PIN (optional)** carries the hint **For clock in/out**. The clock in and clock out themselves are done with the **Clock In** and **Clock Out** buttons in the staff list — the PIN is never entered anywhere as a sign-in credential.

When you edit someone, leaving **Password** blank keeps the current password. The form tells you this with the hint **(leave blank to keep current)**.

### What each role can do here

- **Admin** picks any role and any branch, including the option **All Outlets (Admin)**.
- **Manager** can only create **Staff**, and the branch is fixed to the manager's own branch.
- **Staff** sees no **Role** and no **Assign to Outlet** control. When a staff member edits their own record, the form shows the note **You can only edit your name, email & password**.

The list is filtered to what you may see. A staff member sees only their own row. A manager sees only the staff of their own branch, and never an admin. An admin sees everyone.

### Clock in and clock out

The **Status** column shows whether a person is **Clocked In** or **Clocked Out**. The **Clock In** and **Clock Out** buttons are available to an admin, a manager, or the person themselves.

### Removing a staff member

The trash icon asks **Are you sure you want to delete this staff member?** and then removes the record.

<Note>
Deleting a staff member is permanent. There is no switch to deactivate or suspend someone, so if you only want to stop someone working for a while, leave the record alone and change their password instead.
</Note>

## Outlet Management

The screen is headed **Outlet Management** with the subtitle **Manage your restaurant branches/locations**. Each branch card shows the name, the address and the phone number. The header shows **`{{used}}` / `{{max}}` outlets used**, and adding beyond the plan limit is refused with **Maximum outlets reached (`{{max}}`). Upgrade your plan to add more outlets.**

### Adding a branch

Press **Add Outlet** and fill in the **Add New Outlet** form:

| Field | What you put in it |
|---|---|
| **Outlet Name** | The branch name. Required. |
| **Address** | The full address of the branch. |
| **Phone Number** | The contact number for the branch. |

### Switching between branches

An admin gets a **Select outlet** dropdown in the menu. It lists every branch of the restaurant group. Pick one and the app reloads on that branch. If an admin signs in without a branch, the app picks the first branch automatically.

Everyone else sees a read-only panel with the label **Current Outlet** and the name of their branch.

<Note>
Only an admin can add a branch or switch between branches. A manager and a staff member always work in the branch they are assigned to.
</Note>

### What belongs to a branch

A branch carries its own settings, not just its name and address. The things that change when you switch branch are:

- The currency — symbol, code and decimal places.
- The timezone and the country used for the holiday calendar.
- The tax settings.
- The date format.
- Everything else on the **Settings** screen, including the printer, the kitchen reminder and the order switches.

<Note>
Because the settings belong to the branch, switching branch swaps the whole Settings screen. Check the branch name in the Settings header before you change anything.
</Note>

### Removing a branch

The trash icon asks **Are you sure you want to delete this outlet? This will affect all data associated with it.** and then deletes the branch.
