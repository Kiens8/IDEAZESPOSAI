# Kitchen display system

The kitchen display is the screen your kitchen works from. Every order you send from the POS lands here as a ticket, and the kitchen advances each ticket one step at a time.

Open it from the sidebar with **Kitchen**. Every staff member can reach it. Your plan also has to include the Kitchen feature, otherwise the entry does not appear in the menu.

## How orders reach the screen

The screen refreshes itself every few seconds and pulls in the orders for your outlet. You do not have to press anything to pull in a new order.

Four order statuses sit on the board: **Pending**, **Confirmed**, **Preparing** and **Ready**. An order in any other status is not shown.

Two kinds of order never reach the kitchen board:

- A delivery drops off the board once the driver has picked it up or delivered it.
- An order that the guest has to pay for before you start, but has not paid yet, stays hidden from the kitchen entirely.

<Note>
The board only shows live orders. An order the guest has cancelled, or one that has already been served, is not on it.
</Note>

## The status strip

At the top of the screen is a strip of four cards — **Pending**, **Confirmed**, **Preparing** and **Ready** — each with the number of orders currently in that status. The kitchen can see at a glance where the work is sitting.

## What a ticket shows

Each order sits on its own card. A card shows:

- The **short order number**, a short form of the order number such as `A4821`. This is the number to call out when the food is up.
- A **Paid** badge, when the order has been paid in a way other than paying at the counter.
- The order type — **Dine In**, **Takeaway** or **Delivery**.
- How long the order has been waiting, as **Just now**, **12m ago**, or a clock time.
- A countdown in `M:SS` when the order is being timed.
- A **NEW ITEM (`{{count}}`)** or **NEW ITEMS (`{{count}}`)** badge when more items have been added to the ticket since the kitchen last saw it. The new lines are highlighted in the item list and each one is marked **NEW**.
- The **table number** and the **guest name**.
- The items, with the quantity in a circle, the dish name, and the item modifications listed under it.

<Note>
The card shows the item modifications only. The chosen options and the line note are on the printed kitchen ticket, not on the screen. If the kitchen relies on the screen, ask them to read the printed ticket.
</Note>

Tickets are ordered so the work in hand is at the top: preparing first, then confirmed, then pending, and ready last. Within a status the oldest ticket comes first.

## Advancing a ticket

There is one button on a card and it moves the order one step along:

1. **Confirm Order** — the order moves to confirmed.
2. **Start Preparing** — the order moves to preparing.
3. **Mark Ready** — the order moves to ready.

Once a ticket is ready, the button is gone and the card shows a green **Ready!** panel. There is no served or collected button on this screen. The flow stops at ready.

A ticket that is ready stays on the screen until its status moves on. There is no dismiss button, and nothing clears the board for you.

<Note>
The screen shows an **Auto-dismiss: `{{seconds}}`s** readout, but nothing on this screen auto-dismisses. The readout is not a promise that tickets will clear themselves.
</Note>

The board keeps the last tickets it had if a refresh fails, and raises a banner. The banner only appears in the full screen wall display mode. The ordinary kitchen screen shows no connection-lost indicator, so a frozen screen does not tell you the connection has gone.

## Sounds

The kitchen display makes a noise when something happens:

- A new order rings once.
- New items added to an existing ticket ring the same new-order sound.
- The reminder rings when an order has waited too long.

The sounds are generated by the browser itself. There is no audio file to install and nothing to configure.

A **Sound On** or **Muted** button sits on the board, with the tooltips **Mute notifications** and **Unmute notifications**. This mute only applies to this device, and it resets to unmuted when the screen is reloaded.

<Note>
Switching **Sound notifications** off in your outlet settings does not silence a board that is already open. The settings readout shows **Sound: Off** while the board keeps beeping. Use the **Muted** button on the board itself to stop the noise.
</Note>

## The reminder

Your outlet has a reminder threshold. It is set in **Settings**, in the Kitchen Display tab, as **Order Reminder (minutes)**, and it allows 1 to 30 minutes. If it has never been set, the threshold is 5 minutes.

The reminder only applies to orders that are still **Pending** or **Confirmed**. Once an order moves to **Preparing** or **Ready**, the reminder is cleared for it.

The card carries a countdown in `M:SS` that ticks down to the end of the current cycle and then starts again at the full threshold. When the countdown enters its last minute, the card gets a red pulsing ring and a **REMINDER** badge. The reminder sound plays once per order per cycle.

<Note>
The setting's help text says the alert fires when an order exceeds the threshold. In practice the alert appears in the final minute before the threshold is reached, and it comes back once per cycle. Treat the last minute as your cue to chase the order.
</Note>

## Printing kitchen tickets

### Automatic

If your outlet has automatic kitchen printing switched on, a ticket prints the first time the kitchen sees an order. It uses your outlet receipt printer setup.

An order that only grew — extra items added to a ticket already on the board — is not printed again. It rings the sound and shows the **NEW ITEM** badge only.

### Manual

Every card carries a printer button, titled **Print kitchen ticket**. It prints that one ticket.

### Which printers work

The printing behaviour follows your outlet receipt printer type:

- A **network** printer receives the ticket over the network.
- A **quick** printer opens a browser print dialog with the ticket.
- A **pdf** printer also opens a browser print window with the same ticket. Despite the name, no PDF file is produced.
- Any other printer type prints nothing.

### What the ticket contains

The ticket is headed **KITCHEN** between asterisks, and carries the short order number, the order type, the customer name, the table number, the order time, the full order number, and a list of the items with their quantities and line details. The chosen options are printed in capital letters, one per line, and the line note is printed in italics. A dashed line closes the ticket.

<Note>
If the ticket fails to print, the kitchen is not told. There is no error on the screen and no message in the kitchen, so a missing ticket has to be noticed and reprinted by hand with the printer button on the card.
</Note>

<Note>
A ticket printed on a network printer shows the order type in English — **DINE IN**, **TAKEAWAY** or **DELIVERY** — and its time is not converted to your outlet timezone. The browser-printed ticket does use your outlet timezone.
</Note>

## The wall display

**Full screen** opens the board outside the app shell, edge to edge, on a TV or tablet in the kitchen. Its header holds the **Sound On** / **Muted** toggle, **Refresh** and **Exit**. **Exit** returns to the ordinary kitchen screen with no PIN, so anyone at the board can leave it.

On a screen narrower than 1024 pixels the wall display shows the hint **Open this page on the kitchen display (TV or tablet).** with a dismiss button.
