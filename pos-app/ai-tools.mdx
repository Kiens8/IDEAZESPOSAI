# AI tools

IDEAZES AI reads your own data and turns it into suggestions you can act on. It lives in three places: the **AI Tools** screen, the AI panels on your **dashboard**, and a small upsell suggestion that appears at the counter.

Open it from the sidebar with **AI Tools**. It is for managers and admins, and your plan has to include the AI Tools feature, otherwise the screen does not appear in the menu.

## The AI Tools screen

The **AI Tools** screen is marked **Powered by Ideazes AI**. It has five tabs:

- **AI Insights**
- **Menu Analytics**
- **AI Campaigns**
- **Competitor Discover**
- **Competitor Analysis**

Each tab has its own entitlement. If you do not hold one, the tab is drawn in a locked state and shows the plan it needs — **This feature is available on the `{{plan}}` plan** and **Upgrade to `{{plan}}` to access**. Clicking it shows that locked panel instead of the tool. If you lose an entitlement while a tab is open, you cannot switch away from it.

If you hold none of them at all, an upgrade banner sits above the tabs: **Unlock All AI Tools**, **Get access to all AI features with Pro or Enterprise plans**, and an **Upgrade Now** button.

<Note>
The AI Tools screen is for **manager and admin accounts**. Staff accounts do not see it. Each tab also needs its own plan: **AI Insights** is an Enterprise feature, and **Menu Analytics**, **AI Campaigns**, **Competitor Discover** and **Competitor Analysis** are Pro features. The dashboard AI panels below are a different matter — they follow your role, not your plan.
</Note>

## AI Insights

A summary of your business over a period you pick. The cards are **Total Sales**, **Total Orders**, **Avg Order Value** and **Unique Customers**, and there is a written summary line above them.

Three quick-action cards point you at the other tools: **Menu Optimization**, **Launch Campaign** and **Customer Insights**.

## Menu Analytics

Your menu economics, read and proposed back to you. The app looks at the menu mix and suggests:

- A **Price Increase** on an item that sells well.
- A **Bundle** for an item that sells badly.
- A **Promotion** for an item with no orders at all.

Each suggestion shows a **Confidence: `{{confidence}}`** score and carries two actions: **Apply**, which writes the new price, and **Dismiss**, which leaves the menu alone.

Below the suggestions is the item-by-item table with the columns **Item**, **Cost**, **Orders**, **Revenue** and **Margin**.

<Note>
Menu Analytics works from your dish costs. If your recipes have never been valued, the cost and margin columns have nothing to work from, and the suggestions cannot be trusted. Value your recipes first.
</Note>

## AI Campaigns

A campaign is a voucher mailing to a segment of your customers. The tab asks the app to suggest which segment to target and what conversion to expect, and you apply a suggestion with a click. You can also create one by hand: a type, a trigger, a discount, a minimum spend and a number of valid days.

Campaign types are **Win-back**, **Birthday**, **Promotion** and **VIP**. Trigger types are **Inactivity days**, **Birthday**, **Spend threshold** and **Manual**.

The stats across the top are **Total Sent**, **Conversions**, **Avg Conversion** and **Active Campaigns**, and the table lists each campaign with its **Campaign**, **Trigger**, **Sent**, **Converted**, **Rate**, **Status** and **Actions**. A campaign can be **Pause**d and **Activate**d again, or sent immediately with **Send Now** — the result tells you how many vouchers were sent.

<Note>
Sending a campaign issues real vouchers to real customers. Check the segment and the discount before you use **Send Now**.
</Note>

## Competitor Discover

Find the places around you that compete with your restaurant. You give it a **Location** and a **Radius**, or you tick **Use my location** and let the app find you. The place is looked up, nearby places are found, and each one is analysed for you.

Each result carries its own **AI Insights**, which you can show or hide, and a **Save** action. Saving adds the competitor to your list and lets you record its **Website**.

## Competitor Analysis

This tab works on the competitors you have already saved. It loads your own menu and a competitor's items, compares the two, and has the comparison analysed for you. It can also pull the app's own AI insight for your outlet.

<Note>
**Competitor Analysis** has nothing to work on until **Competitor Discover** has saved at least one competitor.
</Note>

## The AI panels on your dashboard

Four AI panels sit on your dashboard:

- **AI Insights**
- **AI Alerts**
- **Demand Forecast**
- **📊 Order Insights**

Together they cover customer insights, a demand forecast, order insights and alerts that need your attention.

These panels follow your **role**, not your plan. A manager sees them on any plan. A staff account does not see them at all.

## The upsell suggestion at the counter

When you add an item at the counter, the counter asks for related items and offers up to four of them in a popup headed **You might also like...**. Each suggestion carries a reason, for example **Customers who ordered `{{name}}` also liked:**. You can pick one or press **Continue ordering** to dismiss it.

If the app has nothing to suggest, the popup says **No suggestions available**.

<Note>
This is the counter only. The kiosk does not use the popup.
</Note>
