# Delivery

The **Delivery** screen is the staff back office for delivery. Its subtitle reads **Today's deliveries and the driver roster**.

Delivery is switched on per branch, and it is off until you turn it on. A branch with delivery switched off has no delivery screens at all.

<Note>
The back office first checks whether your outlet actually offers delivery. If it does not, the screen says **The delivery is not available at this outlet.** and the tabs never appear.
</Note>

## The three tabs

The screen has three tabs: **Today's Deliveries**, **Drivers** and **Delivery Zones**. Any staff member can open it, and your plan has to include the Delivery Management feature.

### Today's Deliveries

This is the day's delivery orders, each with its status:

- **Unassigned**
- **Assigned**
- **Picked up**
- **Delivered**
- **Cancelled**

The status moves as your staff work the order. On an order that has no driver yet, the action is **Assign driver**. From there the order can move to **Picked up** and then **Delivered**, and an unassigned order can be **Cancelled**. Once a driver is on the order you can change them with **Change driver**, and the order is shown as **Out for delivery**.

A driver link can be produced with **Driver link** and sent on with **Copy link**. The screen confirms it with **The link is ready. It expires in 24 hours.** If no driver is on the order yet, the screen says **Assign a driver first.**

When nothing has come in yet the list says **No deliveries yet today.**

### Drivers

The **Drivers** tab is your driver roster. Each driver has a name, a phone number and a vehicle. You can add a driver, edit one and remove one, and this is where a delivery order is assigned from.

When you have no drivers the tab says **No drivers yet - add one in the Drivers tab.**

### Delivery Zones

The **Delivery Zones** tab prices delivery by postcode area. It appears only when your plan includes the Delivery Zones feature.

## Delivery zones

The panel is headed **Your postcode zones**. A zone holds:

- **Zone name** — for example **KL Central**.
- **Postcodes** — one postcode per line. A shorter code covers everything that starts with it, so **500** covers **50050**.
- **Delivery fee** — the fee for a delivery to those postcodes.

The fee itself is never worked out in the screen. The server answers with the fee, and a postcode is matched to the first active zone that covers it.

Zones can be **Inactive**. You **Activate the zone** or **Deactivate the zone** from the zone itself.

The switch **Only deliver inside my zones** decides what happens outside every zone:

- **On** — a postcode that no active zone covers is refused.
- **Off** — the zones only price the areas they cover, and everywhere else uses the flat fee from your settings.

The switch is off by default.

Removing a zone asks **Remove this zone? The orders already priced by it keep their fee.** Orders that were already priced keep the fee they were given.

<Note>
A zone only prices the area it covers. If you want one flat fee for everywhere, leave the switch off and set the fee in your delivery settings.
</Note>

## What the counter does differently for a delivery

A delivery order is refused before it is sent if any of these is missing:

- **The address** — you get **A delivery needs an address.**
- **A registered customer** — you get **A delivery must be for a registered customer. Pick the customer - the walk-in customer cannot be used for a delivery.**
- **The minimum order** — you get **The bill is below the minimum order of `{{amount}}`.**

<Note>
A delivery always needs a registered customer. The walk-in customer cannot be used for a delivery, so pick the guest in the **Member (earn points)** field before you send it.
</Note>

## Delivery settings

The delivery settings live on the **Delivery** tab of your outlet's settings, and they apply to that one outlet. The section is headed **Delivery Settings** and its subtitle reads **Turn the delivery on for this outlet, set the fee and the coverage, and share the storefront with your own customers.**

### The switch

**Offer delivery at this outlet** turns delivery on for the branch. The hint under it reads: this outlet only, and a customer sees the delivery when the switch is on and the restaurant's plan allows it.

### Fees

- **Delivery fee** — the flat fee for a delivery.
- **Free delivery above** — the bill above which the delivery is free. Leave it empty if you have no free delivery.
- **Minimum order** — the smallest bill you accept for a delivery.

### Coverage

**Delivery radius (km)** is how far you will deliver. A radius of 0 means no limit. When your plan includes Delivery Zones, the zones sit alongside this and price their own areas.

### The money

**Prepaid only** decides when the customer pays. When it is on, every delivery is paid before it is prepared. When it is off, the customer can pay the driver.

**Allowed payment modes** is the list of payment modes a customer may choose, and **The delivery's default mode** is the one used unless the customer picks another. **Pay online** is only offered when a payment account is connected and your outlet is in Malaysia. Otherwise the mode is shown as **not available yet - the restaurant has no payment account connected**. If you allow only one mode, no choice is shown to the customer.

### Delivery time

**Delivery time** holds the shortest and the longest estimate the customer is shown, in minutes. The driver's route refines the estimate.

### Customer registration

This switch asks a customer to register before they order. The screen warns you: **The delivery cannot go live while this is off.**

### Storefront

The **Storefront** part gives your outlet its own delivery link:

- **Storefront slug** — letters, numbers and dashes only, and it has to be unique. Save it with **Save the slug**. If the slug is not allowed the screen says **The slug may only use letters, numbers and dashes.**, and if another outlet already has it, **That slug is already in use by another outlet.** A saved slug confirms with **The slug was saved.**
- **Storefront link** and **Copy the link** — the link you share with your own customers.
- **WhatsApp** — shares the link on WhatsApp.
- **Download the QR** — a branded QR PNG of your delivery page. The screen says **Your QR code**, and asks for a saved slug first with **Save a slug first - the QR carries your delivery page.**

While delivery is off at the outlet, the section shows **The delivery is off at this outlet. Turn the switch on to share the storefront.**
