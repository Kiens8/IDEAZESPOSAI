# Build your menu

Your menu lives in **Menu Management**. This is where you create the categories your staff browse, the dishes inside them, and the sizes and choices a guest can pick.

Open it from the sidebar with **Menu**. The screen is for managers and above. It also needs the Menu feature on your plan, otherwise it does not appear in the sidebar.

## How the menu is arranged

Your menu is two levels deep:

1. A **category** — for example *Mains*, *Drinks*, *Desserts*.
2. A **menu item** inside that category.

The screen shows your items as a grid of cards. Above the grid is a row of filter chips. The first chip is **All**, which shows everything. Each other chip carries a category name and narrows the grid to the items in that category.

Every item card shows:

- the item name and its description
- the price
- a cost and margin line, or an estimated cost line
- an **Available** or **Unavailable** badge
- a **Recipe** badge when the dish has a recipe attached

## Categories

Press **Manage Categories** to open the category list. The panel has one field with the placeholder **New category name** and a plus button. Type a name and press the plus button to create it.

Each category in the list has two buttons:

- The pencil button puts the name back into the field so you can rename it. Type the new name and save.
- The trash button deletes it, after a browser confirmation that reads **Delete this category? Items in this category will need to be reassigned.**

If you have no categories yet, the panel says **No categories yet. Add your first category above!**

<Note>
**Categories cannot be reordered in the app.** There is no drag handle and no up or down arrow. The order you see is the order the categories come back in, and you cannot change it from here.
</Note>

Saving a category needs your outlet to be set. If your account is not linked to a restaurant group yet, the app refuses and tells you **Your account is not linked to a restaurant group yet. Please contact support.** A failed save shows **Failed to save category** and a failed delete shows **Failed to delete category**.

## Adding a menu item

Press **Add Item** to open the item form. The fields are:

| Field | What it is | Required |
|---|---|---|
| **Category** | The category the item belongs to. The empty option reads **Select category**. | Yes |
| **Name** | The name your staff and guests see. | Yes |
| **Description** | A short line under the name. | No |
| **Image URL** | A link to a picture of the dish. The hint reads **Recommended: 800x600px, max 200KB for fast loading**. | No |
| **Price (`{{currency}}`)** | The selling price of the dish. | Yes |
| **Estimated Cost (`{{currency}}`)** | What the dish costs you to make. The hint reads **For margin calculation (optional)**. | No |
| **This is an add-on item** | Marks the item as an extra rather than a dish. | No |
| **Add-on Price (`{{currency}}`)** | The price of the add-on. Only appears once you tick the box above. | No |

Save the item with **Update Item** when you are editing, or **Add New Item** when it is new.

<Note>
There is no tax field on a menu item. Tax is set for the whole outlet in your settings and applied once to the cart subtotal, so you never set tax per dish.
</Note>

<Note>
There is no switch on this form to hide a dish from the customer app, and your stock levels do not hide it either. A dish whose ingredient is out of stock is still offered to guests. To stop a dish being sold, take it off your menu yourself.
</Note>

<Note>
If the save fails, nothing is shown on screen. The item form gives no error message when a save does not go through, so go back to the grid and check whether the item is there before trying again.
</Note>

Deleting an item asks first: **Are you sure you want to delete this item?**

## Sizes and choices

The fork button on an item card opens the **Choices & sizes** panel. The panel header reads **The choices and the sizes of `{{name}}`**, with the line **The sizes a guest can pick and the choices that go with them.**

### The sizes

A size is a version of the dish — Large, Small, Regular. Under **The sizes** you add one with **Add a size**.

Each size takes:

- **The name (Large, Small)** — the label the guest sees.
- **The price difference** — the extra money, or less money, that this size adds to the dish.
- **The cost multiplier** — how much of the ingredients this size uses. **1.5** means half again as much of the ingredients as the base size.

If the dish has no sizes yet, the section says **This dish has no sizes yet.**

### The groups of choices

A group is a named set of options that belongs to the dish — Noodle, Spice, Rice. Under **The groups of choices** you create one with **New group**.

Each group takes:

- **The name of the group (Noodle, Spice)**
- **at least** — the smallest number of options the guest has to pick
- **at most** — the largest number of options the guest may pick

The allowed range is shown as **pick `{{min}}` to `{{max}}`**. A new group starts at 0 and 1, so it is optional and the guest may pick a single option.

A group you already created for another dish can be attached with **Choose a group...** and then **Put it on this dish**. A group already on the dish can be taken off with **Take off this dish**, or deleted outright with the confirm **Delete the group "`{{name}}`"? Every dish that carries it loses it.**

Inside a group, options are added with **Add a choice**. A choice takes:

- **The name (Bihun)**
- **The price difference** — the extra money the option adds
- an ingredient and a quantity, but only if you have the Inventory feature on your plan. If you do not, the panel shows **Linking an ingredient to a choice needs the Inventory feature. The choices and the sizes work without it.**

If the dish has no groups yet, the section says **This dish has no groups of choices yet.**

### Required and optional

The panel has no **Required** checkbox. What decides it is the at least and at most pair:

- **at least 0, at most 1** — optional, and the guest picks one option.
- **at least 0, at most more than 1** — optional, and the guest may pick several options.
- **at least 1 or more** — the guest must make that many picks before the dish can be ordered.

A group that the dish must have also has a required flag on the link between the group and the dish. The picker honours that flag first, and falls back to the group's own minimum when there is no flag.

<Note>
Deleting a group removes it from **every** dish that carries it, not just the one you are looking at.
</Note>

## Add-ons and choices are not the same thing

This trips people up, so it is worth being clear:

- An **add-on** is a menu item of its own. You create it in the ordinary item form by ticking **This is an add-on item** and giving it an **Add-on Price (`{{currency}}`)**. It carries its own price.
- A **choice** is not a menu item. It lives inside a group that is attached to a dish, and it only carries a **price difference** that is added to the dish.

## How the price of a line is worked out

When a dish is ordered, the money on that line is:

1. the price of the dish, or the own price of the size when the guest picks a size that has one, and
2. plus the **price difference** of every choice the guest picked.

The size cost multiplier does not change the money. It changes how much ingredient the kitchen uses, which is what feeds the cost and the margin.

## Cost and margin

Each item card shows the money the dish makes you:

- **Cost: `{{amount}}`** and **Margin: `{{amount}}` (`{{percent}}`%)** when the dish has a recipe. The cost comes from the recipe, and the margin is the price minus that cost.
- **Est. Cost: `{{amount}}`** when the dish has no recipe but you filled in the **Estimated Cost (`{{currency}}`)** field.

<Note>
The recipe cost and the margin only appear when the Inventory feature is on your plan. Without it, only the estimated cost you typed yourself is shown.
</Note>

To build the recipe behind those figures, see [Stock and suppliers](inventory).
