# Manage live orders

The Orders screen is where your team runs the day. It shows every live order, groups them for you, and gives you the buttons you need to move an order along and take the money.

Open it from the sidebar with **Orders**. Every staff member can reach it. Your plan also has to include the Orders feature, otherwise the screen does not appear in the menu.

## The three views

The screen has a strip of three tabs at the top:

- **📋 Queue** — every live order, grouped into kitchen sections.
- **🪑 Tables** — orders grouped by table, for settling a whole table at once.
- **🥡 Takeaway** — the takeaway orders waiting to be handed over. The tab carries a count of how many are waiting.

Under the tabs a line counts the four kitchen buckets for you: **`{{pending}}` pending • `{{confirmed}}` confirmed • `{{preparing}}` preparing • `{{ready}}` ready**.

## The six sections

The queue is split into six sections, in this order:

| Section | What belongs in it |
|---|---|
| **New Orders** | Orders that have just come in and are not confirmed yet |
| **Confirmed Orders** | Orders the kitchen has confirmed |
| **Preparing** | Orders being made right now |
| **Ready to Serve** | Orders finished and waiting to go out |
| **Awaiting Payment** | Orders that have been served and still owe money |
| **Deliveries** | Delivery orders, with their own driver steps |

Deliveries never appear in the kitchen sections. They live in their own section so the kitchen view stays clean.

Cancelled and finished orders drop out of the screen. A prepaid order stays visible until it is handed over, and a pay-at-counter order stays visible until it is paid.

## The buttons on an order

Each order row carries the buttons that make sense for it right now:

- **✓ Confirm** — accepts a new order.
- **Start Preparing** — moves it to the kitchen.
- **Mark Ready** — the food is done and waiting.
- **💰 Record Payment** — takes the money.
- **❌ Cancel Order** — throws the order away.
- **➖ Remove Item** — takes a dish off an order that is still under way.
- **Print Receipt** — appears as a banner once the payment is in.

The next-status button moves the order one kitchen step forward. A prepaid order can only move forward once it has been paid. A pay-at-counter order can only move forward while it is still unpaid.

**💰 Record Payment** appears when the order still owes money and has at least one item that has not been cancelled.

**❌ Cancel Order** is only offered while the order is new, confirmed or preparing, and only while it is unpaid.

**➖ Remove Item** is offered on the same orders, and only when the order has more than one item on it.

## Cancelling an order and removing an item

Both of these ask you why.

**❌ Cancel Order** opens a dialog headed **Please select a reason for cancellation:** with a dropdown of reasons: **Wrong order received**, **Customer changed mind**, **Item unavailable**, **Duplicate order**, **Staff error** and **Other**. The **Confirm Cancel** button stays disabled until you pick one, and the reason is sent with the cancellation.

**➖ Remove Item** opens a dialog that asks **Select items to remove:** and **Reason for removal:**. The **Remove** button on each item stays disabled until you pick a reason.

Both dialogs close with **Done**.

<Note>
Clearing the cart on the counter screen is the one destructive action with no reason and no confirmation. Cancelling an order or removing an item asks for a reason; **Clear** on the cart does not.
</Note>

## Settling a whole table

The **🪑 Tables** view groups the orders by table number. Takeaway orders that carry a table are grouped there too. Each table card shows how much is still unpaid and how many orders are waiting to be paid. When nothing is owed the card says **Table is free**.

You can settle a table in one go only when every order on it that still owes money has been served. If any of them has not been served yet, the card says **All orders must be served before recording payment** and refuses to settle the table.

## Delivery rows

A delivery row shows its own step chip, the driver, the address, the delivery fee and the button for the next delivery step. The delivery steps are:

- **Unassigned**
- **Assigned**
- **Picked up**
- **Delivered**
- **Cancelled**

The button follows the delivery step: you assign a driver from the confirmed stage onwards, the order goes out only once it is ready, and the handover to the guest is what marks it as served. A delivery row never offers a shared **Mark Served** button.

**💰 Record Payment** on a delivery row waits until the food has actually been handed over.

## Served or collected

The last step is worded for your business type, but it is the same step underneath:

| Business type | The button reads |
|---|---|
| Restaurant or bar | **Mark Served** |
| Cafe, fast food or stall | **Mark Collected** |

The order chip that follows uses the same wording: **Served** or **Collected**.

## Empty states

When there is nothing to show, the screen tells you so plainly:

- An empty queue shows **All Caught Up!** and **No active orders at the moment**.
- An empty takeaway view shows **No takeaways waiting** and **Every takeaway has been settled**.
- When nothing is waiting to be paid, the payment search shows **No served orders waiting for payment**.
