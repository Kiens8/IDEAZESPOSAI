# Site

Site consists of two parts: the **public landing page** for marketing and the **customer portal** for managing your subscription and restaurant.

---

## Public Landing Page (site.ideazes.com)

The landing page is where potential customers learn about IDEAZES POS AI and sign up for a free trial.

### Features

- **Hero Section** - Introduction to IDEAZES POS AI
- **Features Overview** - Key capabilities of the system
- **AI Tools** - showcasing AI-powered features
- **Pricing Display** - Available subscription plans
- **Free Trial Signup** - Create new customer account

### Customer Signup Flow
1. Customer visits site.ideazes.com
2. Views features, AI tools, and pricing
3. Clicks "Try Free" or "Get Started"
4. Fills in registration details (name, email, password, restaurant name)
5. Account created with Starter plan (free trial)
6. Redirected to Customer Portal

---

## Customer Portal (site.ideazes.com - Logged In)

After login, customers access the portal dashboard to manage their restaurant and subscription.

### Dashboard Overview

The main dashboard shows a summary of your subscription status:

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Plan** | Current subscription plan (Starter, Basic, Pro, Enterprise) |
| **Outlets** | Number of outlets allowed vs. maximum |
| **Staff** | Number of staff allowed vs. maximum |
| **Status** | Subscription status (active, trial, expired) |

### Subscription Card

Displays detailed subscription information:
- **Plan Name** - Current plan tier
- **Billing Cycle** - Monthly or Annual
- **Next Renewal** - Date of next billing
- **Status** - Active, Trial, or Expired

### Quick Actions

| Action | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Open POS** | Launch the POS Frontend application |
| **Manage Subscription** | View and change subscription plan |
| **View Billing** | Access invoices and payment history |
| **Update Profile** | Edit account and restaurant details |

## Customer Portal Navigation

### Menu Items

| Section | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Overview** | Dashboard with subscription summary |
| **Subscription** | Manage subscription plan and limits |
| **Billing** | View invoices and payment methods |
| **Profile** | Account and restaurant information |
| **Settings** | Outlet and business settings |
| **Support** | Help and contact support |

---

## Subscription Limits

Each plan has limits on:

| Resource | Starter | Basic | Pro | Enterprise |
|---------|---------|-------|-----|------------|
| Outlets | 1 | 2 | 5 | Unlimited |
| Staff | 3 | 10 | 25 | Unlimited |
| Features | Basic | Standard | Advanced | All |
