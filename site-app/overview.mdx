# Site

The site app is the face of IDEAZES POS AI. It has two halves: the **public site** that markets the product, and the **customer portal** where a restaurant manages its plan, its invoices and its profile.

It runs at `site.ideazes.com`, and every page answers in three languages: **English**, **简体中文** and **ភាសាខ្មែរ**.

## The three languages

Every URL carries its language, so a link always lands on the page in the language you expect:

| Locale | URL | Language |
|---|---|---|
| `en` | `site.ideazes.com/en/...` | English |
| `zh` | `site.ideazes.com/zh/...` | 简体中文 |
| `km` | `site.ideazes.com/km/...` | ភាសាខ្មែរ |

The **language selector** in the header switches the page you are on into the other language, and your choice is remembered on the device. English is the default.

## The public pages

| Page | What it is |
|---|---|
| `/` | The landing page — the pitch, the pricing, the AI features and the FAQ |
| `/plans` | The full plan comparison, feature by feature |
| `/about` | Why IDEAZES exists and what the company stands for |
| `/contact` | The contact form |
| `/security` | How the platform is secured |
| `/terms-of-service` | The terms you accept |
| `/privacy-policy` | What is collected, and how it is used |

Each one exists under every locale, so `/en/contact` and `/km/contact` are the same page in two languages.

## The landing page

The landing page is one long page with these sections in order.

**Hero.** "AI-Powered POS System for Modern Restaurants", the tagline "New Generation. Beyond Traditional POS. Built to Beat the Traditional.", and a carousel of the live POS screens. **Start Free 30-Day Trial** takes you to the signup form. **Go to Portal** takes you to your account when you are signed in. The line above the headline counts the restaurants on the platform, and it is read live from the server.

**Pricing.** The four plans, priced per outlet. One switch sets the currency — **USD** or **MYR** — and another sets the cycle, monthly or yearly. The yearly cycle carries a **17% discount** on the monthly price. When a promotion is running, the card carries a **SAVE** ribbon, the promoted price, and the date the offer ends. The promotion covers the **first billing period only** — the next invoice carries the regular price.

**Features.** Two groups: "How You Run The Day" (the **Staff App**, the **Kitchen Display**, the **Order Queue**) and "How You Grow" (**AI Manager**, **AI Insights**, **Open API**).

**AI features.** Twelve, in three groups — for your customers (**Chat Ordering**, **Personalized Picks**, **Smart Search**, **Voice Ordering**), for your staff (**Smart Upsells**, **Kitchen Routing**, **Fraud Detection**, **Staff Insights**) and for your business (**Menu Analytics**, **Competitor Intel**, **Demand Forecast**, **Smart Inventory**).

**FAQ.** Eleven questions, from what a POS system is to what support you get on each plan.

**Footer.** Links to the features, pricing, plan comparison, about and contact sections, plus the legal pages.

The **Compare every feature** link in the pricing section opens `/plans`, which holds the full comparison — eight sections of features side by side, with the notes on what is coming soon and what is Malaysia-only.

## Creating an account

The signup form asks for:

- **Restaurant name**
- **Email**
- **Country** — the country decides which payment methods you can pay with later
- **Phone** — a country code and a number
- **Password** and **Confirm Password** — six characters minimum, and the two must match
- The terms checkbox

The account is created on the spot, and the site sends a **verification email**. You land in the portal straight away, but the portal holds most of itself back until you click the link in that email. See [the customer portal](customer-portal).

## Signing in

`/login` takes an email and a password. **Forgot Password** asks for the email and sends a reset link, and the reset page sets a new password of six characters or more. The verification link lands on `/verify` and signs you in from there.

Signing in from a link that needs an account — an invoice, say — brings you back to that link afterwards.

## The customer portal

The portal is the account half of the site. It has five tabs: **Overview**, **Subscription**, **Billing**, **Profile** and **Support**.

[Read the customer portal](customer-portal).

## Contact

The contact form asks for a name, an email and a message, and optionally a phone number and a subject. It reaches the team at `support@ideazes.com`.
