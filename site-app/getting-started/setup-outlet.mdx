# Set Up Your Outlet

Configure your outlet settings to customize IDEAZES POS AI for your restaurant.

## Accessing Outlet Settings

1. Go to **Settings** in the navigation menu
2. Select **Outlet Settings**

## Basic Information

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Outlet Name | Your restaurant name |
| Address | Physical address |
| Phone | Contact number |
| Timezone | Your local timezone |

## Restaurant Details

Configure how your restaurant appears to customers:

- **Restaurant Name** - Displayed on receipts
- **Logo** - Upload your logo
- **Banner** - Homepage banner image

## Receipt Settings

Customize your receipt content:

| Setting | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Receipt Header | Text at top of receipt |
| Receipt Footer | Text at bottom of receipt |
| Terms & Conditions | Legal terms |
| Return Policy | Refund policy |
| Receipt Copies | Number of copies to print |

## Payment Settings

Configure accepted payment methods:

- Cash
- Credit/Debit Card
- Online Payment
- Pay Later (for subscription plans)
