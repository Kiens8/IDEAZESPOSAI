# Quick start

This is the short path: account, verification, plan, first order.

## Before you start

- A device — a tablet, a computer or a phone
- An internet connection
- A browser. IDEAZES POS AI is a cloud application, so there is nothing to install and nothing to update.

## Step 1: create your account

1. Go to [site.ideazes.com/en/signup](https://site.ideazes.com/en/signup)
2. Fill in your **restaurant name**, **email**, **country** and **phone**
3. Choose a **password** — six characters or more — and confirm it
4. Tick the terms box and submit

The account is created on the spot and you land in the portal.

## Step 2: verify your email

The site sends a verification email straight away. Open it and follow the link — it signs you in.

The portal holds most of itself back until you do this. Until the address is verified, the subscription tab shows **Email Verification Required**, and the plan, outlet and staff figures on the overview read as a dash.

If the email does not arrive, use **Resend Verification Email** in the portal.

## Step 3: choose a plan

Open the **Subscription** tab and work through the wizard: pick a plan, pick a billing cycle, then pick a payment method.

The three-step wizard ends with **Pay Later** or **Credit/Debit Card (Stripe)**. A Malaysian customer in ringgit can also pay by **DuitNow** or **Touch 'n Go** with a QR code.

See [the customer portal](../customer-portal) for the full detail, including what the currency switch does.

## Step 4: open the POS

Press **Open POS** on the portal overview, or go straight to [app.ideazes.com](https://app.ideazes.com). Sign in with the email and password you just created.

Your branch comes from your staff record, so there is no branch picker on the sign-in screen.

## Step 5: take your first order

### For counter service

1. Click **New Order** on the dashboard
2. Search or browse the menu
3. Add the items to the order
4. Click **Record Payment**
5. Print the receipt, if you have a printer set up

### For table service

1. Select a table
2. Add the items to the order
3. Move the order along: **Pending** → **Confirmed** → **Preparing** → **Ready** → **Served**
4. Click **Record Payment** after serving
5. Print the receipt, if you have a printer set up

## Step 6: check the numbers

The dashboard carries the day's figures. For the detail, see [Reports](../../pos-app/reports).
