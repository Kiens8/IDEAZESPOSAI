# Frequently Asked Questions

## General

### What is POS AI?

POS AI is a cloud-based restaurant management system that helps you manage orders, payments, inventory, and customers from one platform.

### What do I need to use POS AI?

- A device (computer, tablet, or smartphone)
- Internet connection
- A POS AI account

### Can I use POS AI on multiple devices?

Yes! POS AI is cloud-based, so you can access it from any device with a browser.

## Subscription & Billing

### How do I upgrade my plan?

1. Go to **Billing** in your account
2. Click **Change Plan**
3. Select your new plan
4. Complete payment

### Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, you can cancel your subscription anytime. Your access continues until the end of your billing period.

### How does proration work?

When you upgrade, you're only charged for the remaining days in your current billing cycle at the new plan price, minus a credit for your unused time on the old plan.

### When is my billing date?

Your billing date is the day you signed up. For example, if you signed up on March 15th, you'll be billed on the 15th of each month.

## Receipt Printing

### What printers work with POS AI?

POS AI supports:
- Any printer that works with your browser (Quick Print)
- ESC/POS thermal printers (Network)
- Bluetooth thermal printers
- PDF export

### Can I print multiple copies?

Yes! Set the number of copies in **Settings** → **Receipt**.

### Why isn't my network printer working?

1. Check the printer IP address is correct
2. Ensure the printer is on your network
3. Try installing the Browser Print Server extension

## Orders

### How do I process a refund?

1. Go to the original order
2. Click **Actions** → **Refund**
3. Confirm the refund amount
4. A receipt will be generated

### Can I split a bill?

Yes! Use the **Split Bill** feature:
1. Select the order
2. Click **Split Bill**
3. Choose your split method (equal, by item, or custom)
4. Assign payment methods

### Can I modify an order after payment?

Orders cannot be modified after payment. For refunds, please process a refund transaction.
