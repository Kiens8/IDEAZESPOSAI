# Subscription Renewal System

IDEAZES POS AI implements a unified renewal system with clear payment deadlines and access control.

:::info
**Applies to Both Billing Cycles**

This renewal system applies to **both monthly and annual** billing subscriptions:
- **Monthly Billing:** 30-day subscription period, 14-day grace period
- **Annual Billing:** 365-day subscription period, 14-day grace period

All logic is identical for both billing cycles. Only the period length differs.
:::

---

## Core Concepts

### Subscription Period

| Item | Definition | Duration |
|------|------------|----------|
| Subscription Period | Active service time | 30 days (monthly) / 365 days (annual) |
| Grace Period | Time to pay invoice | 14 days (default) |

### Key Rule

**Access is blocked when grace period ends if payment is not received.**

---

## Timeline: Monthly Subscription

```
Day 0:  Subscribe / Renew
         |
         subscription_period_start = Day 0
         subscription_period_end = Day 30
         invoice_created = Day 0
         invoice_due_date = Day 14
         |
Day 7:  Warning email (midpoint)
         |
Day 14: Grace period ends (invoice due)
         |
Day 15+: If NOT paid - ACCESS BLOCKED, Invoice = forgiven, Subscription = expired
         |
Day 30: Subscription period technically ends
         (Access already blocked since Day 15)
```

---

## Invoice Status Flow

```
Invoice Created (pending)
         |
         |--- Paid within 14 days ---> PAID
         |
         |--- Not paid by Day 14 ---|
                   |
                   v
              FORGIVEN
                   |
                   v
         Access BLOCKED
         Subscription EXPIRED
```

## Subscription Status Flow

```
ACTIVE / TRIAL
         |
         |--- Paid ---> ACTIVE (next cycle)
         |
         |--- Not paid by Day 14 ---|
                   |
                   v
              EXPIRED
                   |
                   v
         Access BLOCKED
         Customer must resubscribe
```

## Scenario 1: New Subscription - Paid On Time

```
Day 0:   Subscribe (Monthly)
           subscription_status = active
           subscription_period_end = Day 30
           invoice_status = pending
           invoice_due_date = Day 14

Day 5:   Customer pays invoice
           invoice_status = paid

Day 30:  Subscription period ends
           subscription_status = active (auto-renew)
           new invoice created
           new invoice_due_date = Day 44

Day 44:  Grace period ends for Cycle 2
           (same pattern repeats)
```

**Result:**
- Invoice: pending - paid
- Subscription: active
- Access: Full

---

## Scenario 2: New Subscription - Paid Late (Within Grace Period)

```
Day 0:   Subscribe (Monthly)
           subscription_status = active
           subscription_period_end = Day 30
           invoice_status = pending
           invoice_due_date = Day 14

Day 10:  Warning email sent (Day 7)

Day 13:  Grace period ending tomorrow
           Final reminder email sent

Day 14:  Grace period ends
           Customer pays today
           invoice_status = paid

Day 30:  Subscription period ends
           subscription_status = active (auto-renew)
```

**Result:**
- Invoice: pending - paid (Day 14)
- Subscription: active
- Access: Full

---

## Scenario 3: New Subscription - Not Paid (Access Blocked)

```
Day 0:   Subscribe (Monthly)
           subscription_status = active
           subscription_period_end = Day 30
           invoice_status = pending
           invoice_due_date = Day 14

Day 7:   Warning email sent

Day 14:  Grace period ends
           No payment received
           invoice_status = forgiven
           subscription_status = expired
           Access = BLOCKED

Day 15+:  Customer cannot login
           POS shows subscription expired message
```

**Result:**
- Invoice: pending - forgiven (Day 14)
- Subscription: active - expired (Day 14)
- Access: BLOCKED at Day 14

## Scenario 4: Renewal - Paid On Time

```
Day 30:  Previous subscription period ends
           New invoice created for Cycle 2
           subscription_status = active (auto-renew)
           subscription_period_end = Day 60
           invoice_status = pending
           invoice_due_date = Day 44

Day 35:  Customer pays
           invoice_status = paid

Day 60:  Subscription period ends
           (auto-renews to Cycle 3)
```

**Result:**
- Invoice: pending - paid
- Subscription: active
- Access: Full

---

## Scenario 5: Renewal - Not Paid (Access Blocked)

```
Day 30:  Previous subscription period ends
           New invoice created for Cycle 2
           subscription_status = active
           subscription_period_end = Day 60
           invoice_status = pending
           invoice_due_date = Day 44

Day 37:  Warning email sent

Day 44:  Grace period ends
           No payment received
           invoice_status = forgiven
           subscription_status = expired
           Access = BLOCKED

Day 45+:  Customer cannot login
```

**Result:**
- Invoice: pending - forgiven (Day 44)
- Subscription: active - expired (Day 44)
- Access: BLOCKED at Day 44

---

## Scenario 6: Customer Cancels Subscription (Paid)

```
Day 0:   Subscribe (Monthly)
           subscription_status = active
           subscription_period_end = Day 30
           invoice_status = pending

Day 5:   Customer pays invoice
           invoice_status = paid

Day 15:  Customer decides to cancel
           subscription_status = cancelled
           cancelled_at = Day 15
           Access = CONTINUES until Day 30

Day 30:  Subscription period ends
           subscription_status = cancelled (no renewal)
           Access = BLOCKED
```

**Result:**
- Invoice: pending - paid
- Subscription: active - cancelled (Day 15)
- Access: Full until Day 30, then BLOCKED

---

## Scenario 7: Customer Cancels Without Paying

```
Day 0:   Subscribe (Monthly)
           subscription_status = active
           subscription_period_end = Day 30
           invoice_status = pending

Day 15:  Customer cancels (no payment)
           subscription_status = cancelled
           cancelled_at = Day 15
           Access = CONTINUES until Day 30
           (Grace period still running)

Day 14:  Grace period ends (Invoice due)
           invoice_status = forgiven
           (Access already ending at Day 30 anyway)

Day 30:  Subscription period ends
           Access = BLOCKED
```

**Result:**
- Invoice: pending - forgiven
- Subscription: active - cancelled
- Access: Continues until Day 30, then BLOCKED

---

## Scenario 8: Subscription Expired - Customer Resubscribes

```
Day 0:   First subscription
           subscription_status = active

Day 14:  Grace period ends
           No payment
           invoice_status = forgiven
           subscription_status = expired
           Access = BLOCKED

Day 20:  Customer wants to return
           Customer goes to site to resubscribe
           Creates new subscription
           subscription_status = active (NEW)
           New period starts fresh

Day 34:  New subscription period ends (Day 20 + 14 days grace)
           Invoice created
           invoice_due_date = Day 34
```

**Key Points:**
- Old subscription: expired status remains (historical record)
- New subscription: brand new subscription with new ID
- Customer gets fresh start
- Old forgiven invoice stays on record

---

## Scenario 9: Subscription Cancelled - Customer Resubscribes

```
Day 0:   First subscription
           subscription_status = active

Day 15:  Customer cancels
           subscription_status = cancelled

Day 30:  Subscription period ends
           Access = BLOCKED

Day 40:  Customer returns and resubscribes
           New subscription created
           subscription_status = active (NEW)
           Old subscription stays as cancelled (historical)
```

**Key Points:**
- Old cancelled subscription: stays cancelled (no refund implied)
- New subscription: fresh start
- Both subscriptions visible in admin panel
- Customer can have multiple subscriptions over time

---

## Scenario 10: Trial Subscription - Paid On Time

```
Day 0:   Trial subscription starts
           subscription_status = trial
           trial_ends_at = Day 14

Day 7:   Trial ending warning email sent

Day 14:  Trial ends - Customer pays
           invoice_status = paid
           subscription_status = active
           subscription_period_end = Day 44 (Day 14 + 30 days)

Day 44:  First paid period ends
           (auto-renews)
```

**Result:**
- Invoice: pending - paid
- Subscription: trial - active
- Access: Full after payment

---

## Scenario 11: Trial Subscription - Not Paid

```
Day 0:   Trial subscription starts
           subscription_status = trial
           trial_ends_at = Day 14

Day 14:  Trial ends - No payment
           subscription_status = expired
           Access = BLOCKED
           invoice_status = forgiven
```

**Result:**
- Invoice: pending - forgiven
- Subscription: trial - expired
- Access: BLOCKED at Day 14

## Access Control Logic

### Login Flow

```
Customer attempts login
         |
         v
Check subscription status
         |
    ____|____
   |         |
   v         v
ACTIVE    NOT ACTIVE
   |         |
   |     ____|____
   |    |         |
   |    v         v
   |  EXPIRED   CANCELLED
   |    |         |
   |    v         v
   |  BLOCK    Check period
   |         ____|____
   |        |           |
   |        v           v
   |    Period End   Period Active
   |    in future    |
   |        |         v
   |        |      ALLOW with
   |        |      warning
   |        v
   |      BLOCK
   v
Check invoice payment
   |
   v
Paid - ALLOW
Pending - Check grace period
Overdue - BLOCK
```

### Access Decision Table

| Status | Period | Payment | Access |
|--------|--------|---------|--------|
| active | ongoing | paid | ALLOW |
| active | ongoing | pending | WARNING if due soon |
| active | ongoing | overdue | BLOCK |
| trial | ongoing | - | ALLOW |
| cancelled | future | paid | ALLOW (with warning) |
| cancelled | future | pending | WARNING |
| cancelled | passed | - | BLOCK |
| expired | - | - | BLOCK |

## Grace Period Settings

### Admin Settings

| Setting | Default | Description |
|---------|---------|-------------|
| payment_due_days | 14 | Grace period in days |
| overdue_warning_days | 7 | Days before due_date for warnings |

### Warning Timeline

| Days Before Due | Action |
|---------------|--------|
| 7 days | First warning email |
| 3 days | Second warning email |
| 1 day | Final reminder email |
| 0 days | Grace period ends |
| 1+ days | Access BLOCKED |

---

## Email Notifications

| Email | Trigger | When |
|-------|---------|-------|
| Trial Ending | Trial period ending | 7, 3, 1 day(s) before |
| Billing Reminder | Active subscription due soon | 3 days before due_date |
| Final Reminder | Payment due tomorrow | 1 day before due_date |
| Payment Received | Admin marks paid | Immediately |
| Subscription Expired | Grace period ended | Immediately |
| Subscription Cancelled | Customer/admin cancels | Immediately |

---

## Automatic Processes

### Daily Cron Job (9 AM)

1. **Send Warning Emails** - Trial ending, billing reminders
2. **Check Expired Subscriptions** - Block access if grace period passed without payment
3. **Mark Forgiven Invoices** - Invoice status = forgiven
