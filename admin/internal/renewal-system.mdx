# Subscription Renewal System

IDEAZES POS AI implements a unified renewal system with clear payment deadlines and access control.

:::info
**Applies to Both Billing Cycles**

This renewal system applies to **both monthly and annual** billing subscriptions:
- **Monthly Billing:** 30-day subscription period, 14-day grace period
- **Annual Billing:** 365-day subscription period, 14-day grace period

All logic is identical for both billing cycles. Only the period length differs.
:::

---

## Core Concepts

### Key Rule

**Access is blocked when grace period ends (Day 14) if payment is not received.**

### Subscription Period vs Grace Period

| Item | Definition | Duration |
|------|------------|----------|
| Subscription Period | Active service time | 30 days (monthly) / 365 days (annual) |
| Grace Period | Time to pay invoice | 14 days (default) |

**Important:** If grace period ends without payment, subscription becomes EXPIRED regardless of cancellation status.

---

## Timeline: Monthly Subscription

```
Day 0:  Subscribe / Renew
         |
         subscription_period_start = Day 0
         subscription_period_end = Day 30
         invoice_created = Day 0
         invoice_due_date = Day 14
         |
Day 7:  Warning email
         |
Day 14: Grace period ends (invoice due)
         |
Day 15+: If NOT paid - ACCESS BLOCKED, Invoice = forgiven, Subscription = expired
         |
Day 30: Subscription period technically ends
         (Access already blocked since Day 15)
```

## Invoice Status Flow

```
Invoice Created (pending)
         |
         |--- Paid within 14 days ---> PAID
         |
         |--- Not paid by Day 14 ---|
                   |
                   v
              FORGIVEN
                   |
                   v
         Access BLOCKED
         Subscription EXPIRED
```

## Subscription Status Flow

```
ACTIVE
         |
         |--- Paid ---> ACTIVE (next cycle)
         |
         |--- Not paid by Day 14 ---|
                   |
                   v
              EXPIRED (blocked immediately)
```

## Cancellation Logic

### Key Points

1. **Cancellation is recorded** but does NOT override payment obligation
2. **If grace period ends without payment, subscription becomes EXPIRED** regardless of cancellation status
3. **With payment before grace period:** Access continues until period end
4. **Without payment:** Access blocked at grace period end

### Cancellation Decision Table

| Payment | Cancel When | Result |
|---------|-------------|--------|
| Paid | Any time | Access until period end, then blocked |
| Not Paid | Before Day 14 | Cancelled at Day 14 becomes EXPIRED |
| Not Paid | After Day 14 | Already EXPIRED, cancellation has no effect |

## Complete Scenarios

### Scenario 1: New Subscription - Paid On Time

```
Day 0:   Subscribe (Monthly)
           subscription_status = active
           subscription_period_end = Day 30
           invoice_status = pending
           invoice_due_date = Day 14

Day 5:   Customer pays invoice
           invoice_status = paid

Day 30:  Subscription period ends
           Auto-renew to Cycle 2
```

**Result:** Invoice paid, Subscription active, Access full

---

### Scenario 2: New Subscription - Paid Late (Within Grace Period)

```
Day 0:   Subscribe
Day 10:  Warning email sent
Day 13:  Final reminder sent
Day 14:  Grace period ends
         Customer pays today
           invoice_status = paid
Day 30:  Period ends, auto-renew
```

**Result:** Invoice paid (Day 14), Subscription active, Access full

---

### Scenario 3: New Subscription - Not Paid (Access Blocked)

```
Day 0:   Subscribe
Day 7:   Warning email sent
Day 14:  Grace period ends
         No payment received
           invoice_status = forgiven
           subscription_status = expired
           Access = BLOCKED

Day 15+:  Customer cannot login
```

**Result:** Invoice forgiven, Subscription expired, Access blocked at Day 14

---

### Scenario 4: Renewal - Paid On Time

```
Day 30:  Previous period ends
         New invoice created
         subscription_status = active
         subscription_period_end = Day 60
         invoice_due_date = Day 44

Day 35:  Customer pays
           invoice_status = paid

Day 60:  Period ends, auto-renew
```

**Result:** Invoice paid, Subscription active, Access full

---

### Scenario 5: Renewal - Not Paid (Access Blocked)

```
Day 30:  Previous period ends
         New invoice created
         invoice_due_date = Day 44

Day 37:  Warning email sent
Day 44:  Grace period ends
         No payment received
           invoice_status = forgiven
           subscription_status = expired
           Access = BLOCKED
```

**Result:** Invoice forgiven, Subscription expired, Access blocked at Day 44

---

### Scenario 6: Cancel - With Payment

```
Day 0:   Subscribe
Day 5:   Customer pays invoice
           invoice_status = paid

Day 15:  Customer cancels
           subscription_status = cancelled
           cancelled_at = Day 15
           Access = CONTINUES until Day 30

Day 30:  Period ends
           subscription_status = cancelled (no renewal)
           Access = BLOCKED
```

**Result:** Invoice paid, Subscription cancelled, Access until Day 30

---

### Scenario 7: Cancel - Without Payment (Within Grace Period)

```
Day 0:   Subscribe
           subscription_status = active

Day 10:  Customer cancels (no payment)
           subscription_status = cancelled
           cancelled_at = Day 10
           Access = CONTINUES until Day 14

Day 14:  Grace period ends
         No payment received
           invoice_status = forgiven
           subscription_status = expired
           Access = BLOCKED

Day 30:  Period end (already blocked)
```

**Important:** Cancellation does NOT protect from non-payment. At Day 14, subscription becomes EXPIRED.

**Result:** Invoice forgiven, Subscription expired, Access blocked at Day 14

---

### Scenario 8: Cancel - After Grace Period (Already Expired)

```
Day 0:   Subscribe
Day 14:  Grace period ends
         No payment
           invoice_status = forgiven
           subscription_status = expired
           Access = BLOCKED

Day 20:  Customer contacts support to cancel
           subscription_status = expired (already)
           cancelled_at = Day 20 (recorded but no effect)
           Access = BLOCKED (no change)
```

**Result:** Already expired, cancellation recorded but no effect on access

---

### Scenario 9: Subscription Expired - Customer Resubscribes

```
Day 0:   First subscription
Day 14:  Grace period ends
         No payment
           invoice_status = forgiven
           subscription_status = expired
           Access = BLOCKED

Day 20:  Customer returns
         Creates new subscription
           subscription_status = active (NEW)
           New period starts fresh
           New invoice created
```

**Key Points:**
- Old subscription: expired status remains (historical record)
- New subscription: brand new with fresh period
- Old forgiven invoice stays on record

**Result:** Old subscription expired, New subscription active, Full access restored

---

### Scenario 10: Subscription Cancelled - Customer Resubscribes

```
Day 0:   First subscription
Day 15:  Customer cancels (paid)
Day 30:  Period ends
           subscription_status = cancelled
           Access = BLOCKED

Day 40:  Customer returns and resubscribes
           New subscription created
           subscription_status = active (NEW)
```

**Key Points:**
- Old subscription: cancelled status (historical record)
- New subscription: fresh start
- Both subscriptions visible in admin panel

**Result:** Old subscription cancelled, New subscription active, Full access restored

---

### Scenario 11: Trial - Paid

```
Day 0:   Trial subscription
           subscription_status = trial
           trial_ends_at = Day 14

Day 7:   Trial ending warning
Day 14:  Trial ends
         Customer pays
           invoice_status = paid
           subscription_status = active
           subscription_period_end = Day 44

Day 44:  First paid period ends
           Auto-renew
```

**Result:** Invoice paid, Trial converts to active, Access full

---

### Scenario 12: Trial - Not Paid

```
Day 0:   Trial subscription
           subscription_status = trial
           trial_ends_at = Day 14

Day 7:   Trial ending warning
Day 14:  Trial ends
         No payment
           invoice_status = forgiven
           subscription_status = expired
           Access = BLOCKED
```

**Result:** Invoice forgiven, Subscription expired, Access blocked at Day 14

## Access Control Logic

### Login Flow

```
Customer attempts login
         |
         v
Check subscription status
         |
    _____|_____
   |           |
   v           v
ACTIVE      NOT ACTIVE
   |           |
   |       ____|____
   |      |         |
   |      v         v
   |    EXPIRED   CANCELLED
   |      |         |
   |      v         v
   |    BLOCK    Check grace period
   |             ____|____
   |            |           |
   |            v           v
   |         GRACE        GRACE
   |         ENDED        ACTIVE
   |            |           |
   |            v           v
   |          BLOCK      ALLOW
   v                       (with warning)
Check payment
   |
   v
Paid - ALLOW
Pending - WARNING if due soon
Overdue - BLOCK
```

### Access Decision Table

| Status | Payment | Access |
|--------|---------|--------|
| active | paid | ALLOW |
| active | pending (due soon) | WARNING |
| active | overdue | BLOCK |
| trial | - | ALLOW |
| cancelled | paid | ALLOW until period end |
| cancelled | no payment | BLOCK at grace period |
| expired | - | BLOCK |

## Grace Period Settings

### Admin Settings

| Setting | Default | Description |
|---------|---------|-------------|
| payment_due_days | 14 | Grace period in days |
| overdue_warning_days | 7 | Days before due_date for warnings |

### Warning Timeline

| Days Before Due | Action |
|---------------|--------|
| 7 days | First warning email |
| 3 days | Second warning email |
| 1 day | Final reminder email |
| 0 days | Grace period ends |
| 1+ days | Access BLOCKED |

---

## Email Notifications

| Email | Trigger | When |
|-------|---------|-------|
| Trial Ending | Trial period ending | 7, 3, 1 day(s) before |
| Billing Reminder | Active subscription due soon | 3 days before due_date |
| Final Reminder | Payment due tomorrow | 1 day before due_date |
| Payment Received | Admin marks paid | Immediately |
| Subscription Expired | Grace period ended | Immediately |
| Subscription Cancelled | Customer/admin cancels | Immediately |

---

## Automatic Processes

### Daily Cron Job (9 AM)

1. **Send Warning Emails** - Trial ending, billing reminders
2. **Check Expired Subscriptions** - Block access if grace period passed without payment
3. **Mark Forgiven Invoices** - Invoice status = forgiven

---

## Key Rules Summary

| Rule | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Grace period = 14 days | Time to pay invoice |
| Access blocked at Day 15 | If grace period ends without payment |
| Period end at Day 30 | Technical end, access already blocked |
| Cancellation recorded | But does NOT override payment obligation |
| Payment extends nothing | Period end date stays fixed |
