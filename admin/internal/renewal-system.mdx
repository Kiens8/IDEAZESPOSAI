# Subscription Renewal System

IDEAZES POS AI implements a unified renewal system that treats payment due period as a grace period for all payment methods. This ensures consistent, fair handling of subscriptions regardless of payment method.

:::info
**Applies to Both Billing Cycles**

This renewal system applies to **both monthly and annual** billing subscriptions:
- **Monthly Billing:** 30-day subscription period
- **Annual Billing:** 365-day subscription period

All logic (grace period, cancellation, expiration, forgiveness) is identical for both billing cycles. Only the period length differs.
:::

---

## Core Concepts

### Subscription Period vs Grace Period

| Item | Definition | Duration | Purpose |
|------|------------|----------|---------|
| **Subscription Period** | Active service time | 30 days (monthly) / 365 days (annual) | How long customer has access |
| **Grace Period** | Time to pay invoice | 14 days (default) | Buffer time to pay invoice |

**Key Rule:** Payment does NOT extend the subscription period. The period end date is set once at subscription/renewal and remains fixed.

---

## Timeline: Monthly Subscription

```
Day 0:  Subscribe / Renew
        |
        subscription_period_start = Day 0
        subscription_period_end = Day 30
        invoice_created = Day 0
        invoice_due_date = Day 14 (grace period)
        |
Day 7:  Warning email (midpoint)
        |
Day 14: Grace period ends (invoice due)
        |
Day 30: Subscription period ends
        |
        If paid - Access continues, new cycle begins
        If not paid - Access revoked at Day 30
```

## Monthly Subscription Lifecycle

### Cycle 1: New Subscription

| Day | Event | Subscription End | Invoice Due | Access |
|-----|-------|----------------|-------------|--------|
| 0 | Subscribe | Day 30 | Day 14 | Active |
| 14 | Grace period ends | Day 30 | Day 14 | Active |
| 30 | Period ends | Day 30 | - | Blocked or Active if paid |

### Cycle 2: First Renewal

| Day | Event | Subscription End | Invoice Due | Access |
|-----|-------|----------------|-------------|--------|
| 30 | Previous period ends | Day 60 | Day 44 | Blocked or Active if paid |
| 44 | Grace period ends | Day 60 | Day 44 | Active if paid |
| 60 | Period ends | Day 60 | - | Blocked or Active if paid |

## Invoice Statuses

| Status | Meaning | Trigger |
|--------|---------|---------|
| `pending` | Awaiting payment | Invoice created, within grace period |
| `paid` | Payment received | Admin marks paid or Stripe confirms |
| `failed` | Payment attempt failed | Stripe payment failed (will retry) |
| `forgiven` | Written off | Grace period expired, payment not received |
| `cancelled` | Manually cancelled | Admin cancels invoice |
| `refunded` | Payment refunded | Refund issued |

---

## Subscription Statuses

| Status | Meaning | Access |
|--------|---------|--------|
| `trial` | Trial period | Limited access until trial_ends_at |
| `active` | Within subscription period | Full access |
| `expired` | Period ended without renewal | Blocked |
| `cancelled` | Manually cancelled | Access until period end |

## Payment Flow Examples

### Example 1: Pay On Time (Monthly)

```
Day 0:   Subscribe
          period_end = Day 30
          invoice_due = Day 14

Day 10:  Customer pays
          invoice status - paid
          Access continues

Day 30:  Period ends
          New invoice created (Cycle 2)
          period_end = Day 60
          invoice_due = Day 44
```

### Example 2: Pay Late But Within Grace Period

```
Day 0:   Subscribe
          period_end = Day 30
          invoice_due = Day 14

Day 13:  Grace period ending tomorrow
          Warning email sent

Day 14:  Grace period ends
          Customer pays today
          invoice status - paid
          Access continues

Day 30:  Period ends
          New invoice created (Cycle 2)
```

**Key:** Paying within grace period does not extend access. Period end is fixed at Day 30.

### Example 3: Do Not Pay - Grace Period Expired

```
Day 0:   Subscribe
          period_end = Day 30
          invoice_due = Day 14

Day 14:  Grace period ends - NO PAYMENT
          invoice status - forgiven
          (NOT immediately expired - waiting for period end)

Day 30:  Period ends
          subscription status - expired
          Access BLOCKED
```

## Cancellation Examples

### Example 1: Cancel After Paying - Full Access

```
Day 0:   Subscribe
          period_end = Day 30
          invoice_due = Day 14

Day 10:  Customer pays
          invoice status - paid

Day 20:  Customer cancels
          subscription status - cancelled
          Access continues until Day 30 (period end)

Day 30:  Period ends
          Access BLOCKED
          No renewal triggered
```

### Example 2: Cancel Without Paying - Limited Access

```
Day 0:   Subscribe
          period_end = Day 30
          invoice_due = Day 14

Day 20:  Customer cancels (NOT paid)
          subscription status - cancelled
          Access continues until Day 30 (period end)

Day 30:  Period ends
          Access BLOCKED
          invoice status - forgiven
```

**Key:** Cancellation does NOT grant immediate access revocation. Access continues until original period end.

### Example 3: Cancel - Already Expired

```
Day 0:   Subscribe
          period_end = Day 30

Day 35:  Period ended on Day 30
          subscription status - expired
          Access BLOCKED

Day 40:  Customer requests cancellation
          subscription status - cancelled (already expired)
          No change in access
```

## Grace Period Examples

### Within Grace Period (Paid)

```
Day 0:   Subscribe
          period_end = Day 30
          invoice_due = Day 14

Day 7:   Grace period = 7 days remaining
Day 10:  Grace period = 4 days remaining
Day 13:  Grace period = 1 day remaining
         Warning email sent

Day 14:  Grace period ends
Day 14:  Customer pays
         invoice status - paid
         Access OK
```

### Within Grace Period (Not Paid)

```
Day 0:   Subscribe
          period_end = Day 30
          invoice_due = Day 14

Day 14:  Grace period ends
         invoice status - forgiven
         Access continues until Day 30

Day 30:  Period ends
         subscription status - expired
         Access BLOCKED
```

### Outside Grace Period

```
Day 0:   Subscribe
          period_end = Day 30
          invoice_due = Day 14

Day 14:  Grace period ended
         invoice status - forgiven (if not paid)

Day 15+: Access still available until Day 30
         Subscription NOT yet expired
         Just invoice is forgiven
```

**Note:** Grace period expiry does NOT immediately block access. Access is blocked only when subscription period ends.

## Payment Methods and Handling

### Stripe

| Event | Action |
|-------|--------|
| `invoice.created` | Track invoice, set due_date |
| `invoice.paid` | Mark invoice paid, period continues |
| `invoice.payment_failed` | Wait for due_date, not immediate expiration |
| `customer.subscription.deleted` | Mark subscription cancelled |

### Pay Later / Bank Transfer

| Event | Action |
|-------|--------|
| Invoice created | Set due_date = today + grace_period |
| Admin marks paid | Mark invoice paid, period continues |
| Due date passed | Wait for period end |

## Access Control Logic

### Login Flow

```
Customer attempts login
        |
Get subscription status from database
        |
1. No subscription - BLOCK
2. Status = expired - BLOCK
3. Status = cancelled
   a. period_end in future - ALLOW with warning
   b. period_end passed - BLOCK
4. Status = active/trial
   a. payment overdue - BLOCK
   b. payment pending (due soon) - WARNING
   c. payment paid - ALLOW
```

### Cancellation Access Rule

| Condition | Result |
|-----------|--------|
| Cancelled and period end in future | Allow login with warning |
| Cancelled and period end passed | Block (period ended) |
| Expired | Block |

## Grace Period Settings

### Admin Settings

| Setting | Default | Description |
|---------|---------|-------------|
| `payment_due_days` | 14 | Grace period in days |
| `overdue_warning_days` | 7 | Days before due_date for warnings |

### Warning Timeline

| Days Before Due | Action |
|---------------|--------|
| 7 days | First warning |
| 3 days | Second warning |
| 1 day | Final reminder |
| 0 days | Due date - grace period ends |
| 1+ days overdue | Period continues, invoice forgiven |

## Email Notifications

| Email | Trigger | When |
|-------|---------|-------|
| Trial Ending | Trial period ending | 7, 3, 1 day(s) before |
| Billing Reminder | Active subscription due soon | 3 days before due_date |
| Final Reminder | Payment due tomorrow | 1 day before due_date |
| Payment Received | Admin marks paid | Immediately |
| Subscription Expired | Period ended | Immediately |
| Subscription Cancelled | Customer/admin cancels | Immediately |

---

## Automatic Processes

### Daily Cron Job (9 AM)

1. **Send Warning Emails** - Trial ending, billing reminders
2. **Check Expired Subscriptions** - Mark pending invoices as forgiven, expired subscriptions
3. **Handle Cancelled Subscriptions** - Let them expire naturally at period end

---

## Summary Rules

| Rule | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Period is fixed | Set once at subscribe/renewal, NOT extended by payment |
| Grace period | Time to pay (14 days) |
| Access blocked | When period_end passes, not when grace period passes |
| Cancellation | Access continues until original period_end |
| Forgiveness | Invoice marked when grace period passes, access ends when period passes |
