# Malaysia e-Invoice (MyInvois) Integration

**Document Version:** 2.0  
**Created:** 2026-11-04 (v1) · **Updated:** 2026-11-04 (v2 — the submission rules were read out of LHDN's own guidelines: **monthly**, not daily ✓ and three findings that change the design ✓)  
**Status:** **Proposal** — the plan, the rails and the phases; nothing is built yet  
**Scope:** the POS backend (the ERP client), the POS frontend (the counter and the settings), the LHDN MyInvois API  
**Related:** `hitpay-integration.mdx` (the Malaysian payment methods ✓), `multi-currency-money.mdx` (the per-outlet currency ✓ — it decides who may submit ✓), `program-changes/ai-chatbot/ai-chatbot-module.mdx` (the tool layer ✓ — the same read-only discipline ✓), `api-authentication-strategy.mdx` (the tenant rules this feature inherits ✓)

> **v2 changed three things.** The consolidated e-Invoice is **monthly**, submitted within
> **7 calendar days after the month-end** — not daily ✓ (§3.6.2 ✓). A single transaction
> above **RM10,000** needs its **own** e-Invoice, in every phase ✓ (Table 3.6 ✓). And the
> buyer may ask for an e-Invoice **later, within the month of the transaction** ✓ (§3.6.8 ✓)
> — which is the whole reason the monthly cut-off exists, and which the POS must support.

## Overview

Malaysia's e-Invoice mandate is live. **No industry is exempt** — restaurants included ✓.
This document is the plan for making the POS an e-Invoice-compliant ERP for our Malaysian
customers: the tax invoice the guest asks for at the counter, and the monthly consolidated
e-Invoice that covers the walk-in sales nobody asked a receipt for.

Two things make this a POS problem and not an accounting problem:

1. **The volume is per transaction.** A restaurant serving 200 covers a day would need 200
   validated e-Invoices a day ✓. LHDN's answer is the **consolidated e-Invoice**: one
   document for all the B2C sales of a **month** ✓.
2. **The clock starts at the table.** The guest asks for a tax invoice *while the coffee is
   still hot* ✓. If the POS cannot produce one at the counter, the restaurant sends someone
   to LHDN's portal by hand — and then does not buy a POS that cannot do it ✓.

### The shape of the solution

```
the order is paid
   ├── the guest did NOT ask for a tax invoice → a normal receipt
   │      └── the sale joins the MONTHLY CONSOLIDATED e-INVOICE (by the 7th of the next month)
   ├── the guest DID ask for a tax invoice → an INDIVIDUAL e-INVOICE
   │      ├── the guest gave a TIN → validate it (once, then cache it) → the document
   │      │      carries the buyer's details
   │      ├── the guest gave only a MyKad / MyPR / MyKAS → the General TIN + the id number
   │      ├── the guest gave only a passport (a foreigner) → EI00000000020 + the passport
   │      └── the guest gave nothing → they get a receipt, and the sale is consolidated
   └── the guest asks LATER (within the month) → the order leaves the batch and is
          invoiced individually (the retro-request)
```

## Who is in scope

| Annual turnover / revenue | Mandatory from |
|---|---|
| Above RM100M | **1 Aug 2024** ✓ |
| RM25M – RM100M | **1 Jan 2025** ✓ |
| RM5M – RM25M | **1 Jul 2025** ✓ |
| Up to RM5M (operating in FY2022) | **1 Jan 2026** ✓ |
| RM3M+ and started 2023–2025 | **1 Jul 2026** ✓ |
| Started 2026 onwards | 1 Jul 2026 or the commencement date ✓ — the **second-year rule** applies if the first-year revenue is expected below RM3M ✓ |
| **Below RM3M** | **exempt** ✓ — unless a shareholder, holding company, related company or joint-venture partner with a turnover of at least RM3M pulls them in ✓ |

**Phase 4 (up to RM5M) has a relaxation period until 31 December 2027; full enforcement
from 1 January 2028** ✓. And the **SVDP** (Special Voluntary Disclosure Programme) lets
missed or erroneous e-Invoices be **back-filed without penalty** from 7 July 2026 to
31 December 2027 ✓ — using the **"SVDP 1.2"** (unsigned) or **"SVDP 1.3"** (signed)
document versions ✓.

> **Read this before selling it.** Most of our restaurant customers are **under RM3M and
> therefore exempt** ✓. The e-Invoice is not a compliance deadline for them — it is a
> **differentiator** for the chains that cross RM3M ✓, for the ones growing towards it ✓,
> and for the ones whose *customers* (corporate accounts, hotels, canteens) demand an
> e-Invoice from them ✓. Sell it as readiness, not as a threat ✓.

**No sector is exempt** ✓ — the tourism, hospitality and F&B industries are in scope like
everything else ✓. The F&B-specific guidance is about *how* (the consolidated mechanism ✓),
not *whether* ✓.

## How MyInvois works

The POS becomes an **ERP client** ✓. LHDN does not push invoices at us — we push documents
at LHDN and they validate them ✓.

1. **Authenticate.** OAuth 2.0 client credentials against the identity service → a bearer
   token valid for **60 minutes** ✓.
2. **Submit.** One or more documents, each a **base64-encoded UBL 2.1 JSON** plus its
   **SHA-256 hash** and a **code number** (our reference) ✓. The API answers **202** with a
   `submissionUID` and the accepted/rejected document IDs ✓ — *accepted*, not *valid* ✓.
3. **Wait.** Validation is asynchronous ✓. We poll **Get Submission** until the submission
   is `valid` or `invalid` ✓. In practice the validated document comes back in **under two
   seconds** ✓ (the guideline's own POS flow ✓).
4. **Show.** A valid document gets a UUID ✓; the receipt carries the **LHDN validation QR**
   so the guest can check it themselves ✓.

### The APIs we use

| API | Method & path | RPM | What it is for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Login as Taxpayer System | `POST /connect/token` | **12** | the client credentials → the token (60 min) ✓ |
| **Login as Intermediary System** | `POST /connect/token` | 12 | **our model** — we act on behalf of a taxpayer ✓ (see below) ✓ |
| Submit Documents | `POST /api/v1.0/documentsubmissions/` | **100** | the documents ✓ |
| Get Submission | `GET /api/v1.0/documentsubmissions/{uid}` | **300** | the polling ✓ |
| Get Document | `GET /api/v1.0/documents/{uuid}` | 60 | the detail ✓ |
| Cancel Document | `PUT /api/v1.0/documents/state/{uuid}/state` | 12 | a mistake, within **72 hours** of validation ✓ |
| Validate Taxpayer's TIN | `GET /api/v1.0/taxpayer/validate/{tin}?idType=&idValue=` | 60 | the buyer's TIN — **validate once, then cache** ✓ |
| Get Document Type | `GET /api/v1.0/documenttypes/{code}` | — | the structure + the workflow parameters (the cancel window) ✓ |

> The rate limit is **per Client ID** ✓ — one Client ID per tenant keeps our traffic
> separate, but a busy tenant cannot starve another one ✓.

### Two ways to be connected — and which one we pick

| | **Direct** (the tenant's own ERP credentials) | **Intermediary** (our platform) |
|---|---|---|
| The credentials | each tenant holds their own `client_id` / `client_secret` ✓ | **we** hold one set ✓ |
| The onboarding | every tenant registers with LHDN and authorises their ERP ✓ | every tenant authorises **us** as their representative ✓ |
| Our effort per tenant | high — registration, credentials, testing ✓ | low — one authorisation per tenant ✓ |
| The risk | the tenant's key sits in our database ✓ | we are on the hook for every tenant's document ✓ |
| Fits | the big chains with their own ERP ✓ | **the long tail — our customers** ✓ |

> **We are a "non-Peppol technology provider"** ✓ (e-Invoice Guideline v2.0 ✓) — the model
> the market's integrators use ✓. **Recommendation: the intermediary model** ✓ — one LHDN
> credential for the platform ✓, one authorisation per tenant ✓, and the tenant's own
> credentials stay theirs ✓. The **direct model** is the fallback for a chain that insists
> on holding its own key ✓ (or for a tenant that runs its own ERP next to our POS ✓).

## The document we build

**UBL 2.1 JSON** ✓, `Invoice` version **1.0** (no digital signature) or **1.1** (signed) ✓.
**Start with 1.0** ✓ — the only difference between them is the signature, and a signature
is a whole project on its own ✓. 1.1 is the target once the pipeline is proven ✓.

### The code tables we must respect

| Table | What we use |
|---|---|
| Tax types | `01` sales tax, `02` service tax, `03` tourism tax, `06` not applicable, `E` exemption ✓ |
| e-Invoice types | `01` invoice, `02` credit note, `03` debit note, `04` refund note, `11`–`14` self-billed ✓ |
| Currency | `MYR` ✓ — and the **exchange rate** when it is not ✓ |
| Unit of measurement | UN/ECE Rec 20 ✓ — `C62` (one) ✓, `EA` (each) ✓, `H87` (piece) ✓, `KGM` (kilogram) ✓ |
| Country | `MYS` ✓ |
| State | the 14 states + 3 federal territories ✓ (`CountrySubentityCode`, e.g. `10` ✓) |
| Payment modes | `01` cash … `08` others ✓ |
| Classification / MSIC | the item's classification code ✓ and the restaurant's MSIC code ✓ |

Every one of these is validated by the **Code Validator** ✓ — a wrong code is a rejected
document, not a warning ✓. The tables are **seeded into the backend from the SDK's JSON
files** ✓ — never typed by hand ✓.

### The validators we will meet

| Validator | What it rejects | How we stay out of trouble |
|---|---|---|
| Structure | the document does not match the version's schema ✓ | build the JSON from the **Get Document Type** schema ✓, and validate it locally before sending ✓ |
| Core fields | the mandatory fields are missing ✓ | the buyer always has a name, an address and a contact number ✓ |
| Taxpayer | the seller/buyer TIN is invalid at the issue date ✓ | validate the buyer's TIN **when it is saved** ✓, not at the counter ✓ |
| Referenced documents | a credit note points at an invalid invoice ✓ | the credit note carries the original's UUID ✓ |
| Code | a code is not in the table ✓ | the code tables are **seeded** ✓ |
| **Duplicate document** | the same `codeNumber` submitted twice ✓ | the code number is immutable and unique per taxpayer ✓ — a retry **never** changes it ✓ |
| **Currency** | the wrong currency / a missing exchange rate ✓ | **only MYR outlets may submit** ✓ — see below ✓ |

> **The currency validator is a real constraint on us.** Demo Cafe 1 bills in **USD** ✓ and
> Kens SS17 in **RM/MYR** ✓ — and they are the **same tenant** ✓. MyInvois is a Malaysian
> system ✓: a USD invoice either fails the currency validator or needs an exchange rate and
> a foreign currency code ✓. **The rule: an outlet submits e-Invoices only when its
> `currency_code == "MYR"`** ✓. The USD outlet keeps its receipts ✓; the MYR outlet gets the
> e-Invoices ✓. The tenant-scoped design makes this a per-outlet flag, not a per-tenant one ✓.

## The three flows

### A. The individual tax invoice (on request)

```
the guest asks for a tax invoice at the payment
   → the payment panel shows a "Tax invoice" toggle + the buyer's fields (Table 3.1)
   → the guest's TIN is validated once (Validate Taxpayer's TIN) and cached
   → the order is marked e_invoice_requested = true
   → the order is queued for submission
   → the worker builds the UBL JSON, submits it, polls the submission
   → the validated document comes back (in practice, under 2 seconds ✓)
   → the receipt carries the LHDN validation QR ✓
```

**The counter interaction may take up to 60 seconds** ✓ (the guideline's own POS flow ✓) —
so asking the guest for their details at the table is the intended design ✓, not an
excuse ✓.

**The guest must never be told it is done when it is not** ✓. If the validation is slow or
LHDN is down ✓, the receipt prints with the reference and the status ✓ and the document
arrives later ✓ (the reprint ✓). The POS shows the validated document **to the cashier** ✓
— that is the guideline's flow ✓.

### B. The retro-request (the guest asks later)

**§3.6.8** ✓ — the buyer may ask for an e-Invoice **any time within the month of the
transaction** ✓. That is exactly the window the monthly batch needs ✓.

So the POS needs a **"request an e-Invoice for a past order"** path ✓:
- from the **order history** ✓ (the manager searches the order ✓ and issues the invoice ✓)
- or from the **customer portal** ✓ (the loyalty guest finds their order ✓ and requests it ✓) ✓ — this is a nice fit for the customer portal we already have ✓
- the order **leaves the monthly batch** ✓ and is invoiced individually ✓
- the request after the month-end is too late ✓ — the POS says so plainly ✓

### C. The monthly consolidated e-Invoice

- Every order that was **not** individually invoiced joins the month's batch ✓.
- The batch is submitted **within 7 calendar days after the month-end** ✓ (§3.6.2 ✓) —
  by the **7th** ✓.
- **The consolidation method is ours to choose** (§3.6.3 ✓):
  - **(a) each receipt's summary as a separate line item** ✓ — the clearest for a reviewer ✓
  - **(b) the receipts as line items in a continuous receipt-number chain** ✓ — the
    smallest document ✓ (a break in the chain starts a new line ✓)
  - **(c) per branch or location** ✓ — **each outlet submits its own consolidated
    e-Invoice** ✓ ✓ — which is the natural fit for our multi-outlet tenant ✓
- **The receipt reference number must be on every line** ✓ (Table 3.5 ✓) — our order
  number is the audit trail ✓.
- **The buyer is `General Public` / `EI00000000010`** ✓ and the rest is `NA` ✓ (Appendix 2 ✓).
- **The limits**: 5MB per submission ✓, 100 e-Invoices per submission ✓, 300KB per
  e-Invoice ✓ — so a busy month is **split into several documents** ✓ (§3.6.4 ✓).
- **Never consolidated**: a transaction above **RM10,000** ✓ (its own e-Invoice ✓), B2B ✓,
  and the industries in Table 3.6 ✓.

> **Monthly, not daily** ✓ — and the daily idea is not merely unnecessary ✓: the monthly
> cut-off is what gives the buyer the right to ask for an individual invoice afterwards ✓
> (§3.6.8 ✓). A daily submission would destroy that right ✓.

### D. The credit note (the voided order)

An order that was already invoiced and must be reversed:
- **Within 72 hours** of the validation → `Cancel Document` ✓ (free, no document) ✓.
- **After 72 hours** → a **credit note** (`InvoiceTypeCode 02`) that references the
  original's UUID ✓ (the Referenced Documents Validator checks it ✓).

The POS's "cancel order" action must therefore know whether an e-Invoice exists for that
order ✓ — the cancel path cannot simply mark the order cancelled and walk away ✓.

### The worker

- A **background task** ✓ (the backend already runs scheduled jobs ✓) that picks the
  `queued` documents ✓, builds them ✓, submits them ✓ and polls them ✓.
- **The monthly batch job runs on the 1st–7th of the month** ✓ — and the operator sees
  the deadline and how many sales are still unbatched ✓.
- The **token is cached per tenant** ✓ and refreshed at 55 minutes ✓ — **never** a token per
  call ✓ (the SDK names this as the #1 anti-pattern ✓).
- The **429** answers carry a `Retry-After` ✓ — we honour it ✓.
- The **backoff** on a failure is exponential ✓.
- A submission is **never re-sent** because we did not hear back ✓ — the
  duplicate-document validator would reject the second copy ✓ and the API would start
  throttling us ✓.

## The frontend

### The settings (`/settings` → **E-Invoice**, admin only ✓)

- The toggle ✓, the TIN + the id type ✓, the registration number ✓, the SST and TTX
  numbers ✓, the MSIC code ✓, the registered address ✓.
- The **client id** and the **client secret** — entered once ✓, stored encrypted ✓, **never
  sent back to the browser** ✓ (the API returns `has_credentials: true` and nothing else ✓).
- The **"Test connection"** button ✓ that logs in and reports ✓ — the SDK's anti-pattern
  list warns against pointless login attempts ✓, so this button is the only one ✓.
- The **sandbox** switch ✓ — the credentials are different on each side ✓, and a restaurant
  must never test against production by accident ✓.
- The **consolidation method** (§3.6.3 ✓) and the **consolidated toggle** ✓.

### The payment panel ✓

- A **"Tax invoice"** toggle ✓ (only when the outlet is enabled ✓ and the currency is MYR ✓).
- The **buyer's fields** ✓ (Table 3.1 ✓): the name ✓, the TIN ✓ (or the MyKad / MyPR /
  MyKAS number ✓, or the passport ✓), the address ✓, the contact number ✓, the SST number ✓.
- The **TIN is validated as it is typed** ✓ (debounced ✓, cached per tenant ✓).
- The **RM10,000 guard** ✓ — an order above RM10,000 **cannot** be consolidated ✓, so the
  toggle is forced on ✓ and the POS says why ✓.
- The toggle **survives the split bill** ✓ — one tax invoice per order ✓, not per split ✓.

### The receipt ✓

- The **LHDN validation QR** once the document is valid ✓, with the document's UUID ✓.
- The **"e-Invoice submitted, validation pending"** line while it is in flight ✓ — the guest
  must never be told it is done when it is not ✓.
- The **reprint** from the order ✓.
- The **consolidated batch's receipt reference** ✓ — so the guest's paper matches a line in
  the monthly document ✓.

### The orders screen ✓

- An **e-Invoice column** with the status ✓ — `not required` / `queued` / `submitted` /
  `valid` / `invalid` ✓.
- An **invalid document** shows the reason and offers a **retry** ✓ — the retry reuses the
  same `codeNumber` and the same payload ✓.
- A **cancel** action on a document that is still inside its 72-hour window ✓.
- A **"request an e-Invoice"** action on a paid order **within the month** ✓ (the
  retro-request ✓).

### The monthly batch screen ✓

- The **period** (the month ✓), the **count of unbatched sales** ✓, the **total** ✓.
- A **"submit now"** button ✓ — but the deadline is visible and automatic ✓.
- The **submitted batches** with their status ✓.

### The customer portal ✓

- The guest finds their order and **requests an e-Invoice** ✓ — within the month ✓ (the
  retro-request ✓). This is the natural home for it ✓ — we already have the portal ✓.

## The rails

The **audit recipe** applies to every route this feature adds ✓:

1. **Who may call it?** → `require_permission("einvoice:read")` /
   `require_permission("einvoice:write")` ✓ (the admin role ✓) and
   `require_staff_permission("staff:einvoice:read")` ✓ for the counter's read-only view ✓.
   The **role table** is `ROLE_PERMISSIONS` in `backend/app/middleware/auth.py` ✓.
2. **Whose data may it touch?** → the tenant helpers in `backend/app/core/tenant.py` ✓ —
   `require_outlet_tenant` for the settings and the documents ✓. The **tenant comes from
   the session** ✓, never from a query parameter ✓.
3. **Did the tenant buy it?** → `require_plan_feature("E-Invoice")` ✓ — the plan cache reads
   it live ✓.
4. **Does the UI agree?** → the nav item's `minRole` + `feature` in
   `frontend/src/config/navItems.js` ✓ and the settings tab's gate ✓.

The same discipline for the frontend ✓: check `res.ok` **and** `Array.isArray(...)` before
`.filter` / `.map` ✓; skip the call entirely when the feature is off ✓.

### The credentials ✓

- The **client secret** lives **in the backend only** ✓ — never in the browser ✓, never in
  the receipt ✓, never in a log line ✓.
- Encrypted at rest ✓ (the same treatment as the other secrets ✓).
- The **token cache is per tenant and in memory** ✓ — a restart just means one extra login ✓.
- The **sandbox and production credentials are separate** ✓ so a test can never touch
  production ✓.
- In the **intermediary model** ✓ the credentials are **ours** ✓ — the tenant's
  authorisation is a flag ✓, not a secret we hold ✓.

## The test plan

### The sandbox first ✓

LHDN runs a **sandbox** ✓ with its own credentials ✓ (a lower rate limit ✓, and the data
is deleted after 3 months ✓). **Nothing is written to production until the sandbox is
green** ✓.

### The local checks (before a document ever leaves ✓)

- The **JSON matches the version's schema** ✓ — checked against the structure from
  `Get Document Type` ✓.
- The **codes are in the tables** ✓ — the tax type ✓, the unit ✓, the country ✓, the state ✓,
  the payment mode ✓, the classification ✓, the MSIC ✓.
- The **currency is MYR** ✓.
- The **buyer has a name, an address and a contact number** ✓.
- The **code number is unique** for the taxpayer ✓.
- The **hash matches the payload** ✓.
- The **amount fields are within 26 digits** ✓ and the **PASSPORT identifier within 12
  characters** ✓ (MyInvois's own limits since 24 October 2026 ✓).
- A **transaction above RM10,000** is never queued for consolidation ✓.

## The open questions

1. **The QR code endpoint.** The SDK lists a "Taxpayer's QR Code" API (60 RPM ✓) — the exact
   path and the payload must be confirmed against the SDK page before the receipt work
   starts ✓.
2. **The structure of v1.0 in practice** — the field paths must be taken from
   `Get Document Type` ✓ (the SDK serves the structure definition ✓) rather than guessed
   from the UBL spec ✓.
3. **The intermediary onboarding** — how a tenant authorises our ERP client in the MyInvois
   portal ✓, and whether the authorisation is per tenant or per document type ✓.
4. **The VAT** — the VAT regime replaces SST ✓; the tax type codes will change with it ✓ —
   the code tables are seeded ✓, so this is a deploy ✓, not a code change ✓.
5. **The payment mode for DuitNow by QR** — `03` (bank transfer ✓) or `06` (e-wallet ✓).
