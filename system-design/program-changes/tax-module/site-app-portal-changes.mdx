# SaaS Tax Module — `site` app and portal changes

> **Date:** November 27, 2026
> **Status:** Proposal — not implemented
> **Author:** Lumi

Surface 3 of 3. The shared contract lives in [tax-module](./tax-module); the endpoints
it reads are in [website-api-changes](./website-api-changes). This document is only
about what changes in the customer-facing Next.js app.

Live source inspected: `POS_AI` @ `d83d6bf`.

---

## 1. What the site does today

| Fact | Where |
|---|---|
| The published plans are fetched from `GET /api/plans/published?locale=` | `site/src/app/[locale]/plans/page.tsx:48` |
| The API base is `NEXT_PUBLIC_API_URL`, defaulting to `https://apisite.ideazes.com` | `plans/page.tsx:14` |
| The invoice list renders `formatCurrency(invoice.amount, invoice.currency)` | `site/src/app/[locale]/portal/billing/page.tsx:462` |
| The invoice detail modal renders the same | `portal/billing/page.tsx:556` |
| The payment panel shows the **amount due** | `site/src/components/PaymentPanel.tsx:220-224` |
| The portal invoice page renders the invoice | `site/src/app/[locale]/invoice/[id]/page.tsx` |
| The datetime helper reads an offset-less datetime as **UTC** and shows it in the **reader's** clock | `site/src/lib/datetime.ts` |
| The portal copy lives in **three** locales: `en`, `km`, `zh` | `site/src/i18n/messages/{en,km,zh}/portal.json` |

### The consequence that shapes this document

The site shows **the gross** today — one number, what you pay. The tax module gives
the customer **three** numbers (net, tax, gross) where there was one.

That is only acceptable if the extra numbers are **legible**. A tax line the customer
cannot read is worse than no tax line: it looks like an unexplained charge. So the
site's job is not to *show more numbers*, it is to make the gross **explainable** —
in the customer's own language.

And the three locales are not a translation chore here. **"SST" is a Malaysian tax
term.** In Khmer and Chinese, the portal either says the same acronym (and a
Cambodian or Chinese reader has no idea what it means) or it uses the local tax name
(which for an export-exempt customer would be *wrong*). That decision is part of the
proposal, and it is a question for the accountant too: what is the term on a tax
invoice for a customer outside Malaysia?

---

## 2. The rule for the customer-facing side

> **The customer sees exactly one number that they must pay, and enough explanation
> to account for it. Never our internal uncertainty.**

Three consequences:

1. The **gross** is always the headline. It is what they pay.
2. The tax line appears **only** when tax was actually charged. A customer under
   `export_exempt` or `export_pending` sees nothing — no "0%", no "tax exempt", no
   footnote.
3. `export_pending` never appears in the site. Not as a badge, not as a note. The
   uncertainty is ours, not theirs.

---

## 3. The price display — `site/src/app/[locale]/plans/page.tsx`

### 3.1 The plan price

Under the recommended **exclusive** basis the displayed price does not change. What
changes is that a note appears beneath it, once the tax is active:

```
Starter
RM 100.00 / month
+ SST
```

The `+ SST` is not decoration — the customer must be told the price is not the total
before they commit. A price that turns into a bigger number at checkout is the kind
of thing that produces a chargeback and a bad review.

Under the **inclusive** basis the price is the gross and the note changes to
`incl. SST` — no number change at all.

Both variants are driven by the `tax` block on `GET /api/plans/published`:

```json
"tax": { "mode": "display", "basis": "exclusive", "rate": 0.08, "label": "SST" }
```

### 3.2 While the tax is not active

While `tax_module_enabled` is off, or the mode is `off`/`shadow`, there is **no note
at all**. Saying "excl. SST" when we charge none is noise, and it invites the
question we cannot yet answer.

### 3.3 The promotional price

The promotion price shows the same annotation as the regular price, and the two
annotations must not contradict each other. The site's price block is a small shared
component; both the regular and the promotional price go through it.

## 4. The checkout and payment panel — `site/src/components/PaymentPanel.tsx`

The panel shows the **amount due** and takes the payment. This is the moment the
gross becomes real, so the breakdown must be **above** the pay button, not after it.

```
Amount due                RM 108.00
  Subtotal                RM 100.00
  SST 8%                  RM   8.00
```

The **amount actually sent to the backend** is the **gross** — `payment.amount` is
what the payment provider charges, and that does not change under the module. What
changes is that the panel can now *show its working*.

Two things that must not go wrong:

- **The displayed total must equal the charged total.** The panel's total is computed
  from the same `subtotal + tax_amount` the API returns, not from a client-side
  re-addition of the plan price. A rounding difference between the shown total and the
  charged total is a support ticket every single time.
- **The tax line is not editable and not optional.** It is a display of what the API
  computed, never a client-side calculation.

For an exempt or pending customer the panel shows the amount due and **nothing else** —
the panel must not render an empty "SST 0.00" row.

## 5. The portal billing page — `site/src/app/[locale]/portal/billing/page.tsx`

### 5.1 The invoice list

The row keeps the gross as its number. A tax line appears under it only for invoices
that carry tax:

```
RM 108.00                          Paid
  SST 8% · 1 Nov – 30 Nov 2026
```

The **service period** is worth showing here. "What did I pay for, and for when?" is
the question a customer opens the billing page to answer, and right now the portal
cannot answer it at all.

### 5.2 The invoice detail modal

The modal gains the same breakdown as the printable invoice:

```
Amount paid           RM 108.00
  Subtotal            RM 100.00
  SST 8%              RM   8.00
Service period        1 Nov 2026 – 30 Nov 2026
```

### 5.3 The portal dashboard — `site/src/app/[locale]/portal/page.tsx`

The subscription card shows the plan and the next renewal. Under the exclusive basis
it may note that the renewal will include the tax — but only if the tax is active. A
customer should not be surprised by the renewal amount, and the portal is where they
look before it renews.

## 6. The printable invoice — `site/src/app/[locale]/invoice/[id]/page.tsx`

The customer-facing document. This is the one that must survive being printed and
handed to someone else.

```
IDEAZES
Invoice IDEZ-2026-0042

Billed to
  Demo Cafe 1
  Cambodia

Invoice date        1 Nov 2026
Due date            1 Nov 2026
Service period      1 Nov 2026 – 30 Nov 2026

Description                     Amount
Starter - monthly subscription   RM 100.00
                                  SST 8%    RM 8.00
                                  Total    RM 108.00

Total due                       RM 108.00

SST registration number  W16-1234567-03
```

Requirements:

- The **service period** is on the document.
- The **tax line** shows the rate and the amount, and the total is the sum of the two
  — the customer's eye goes to the total, and it must reconcile with the amount that
  was charged.
- The **SST registration number** is printed when we are registered.
- The **customer's country** is printed (it is on the invoice for tax purposes), and
  the dates are the customer's own dates.

### 6.1 The dates — `site/src/lib/datetime.ts`

The existing helper reads an offset-less datetime as UTC and shows it in the reader's
clock. That is correct for **moments** (when the invoice was written). It is **wrong**
for a **service period**, which is a range of calendar days, not a moment.

A service period that ends on 30 November ends on the 30th in the customer's clock. It
must not render as "29 Nov" because the end timestamp was `2026-12-01T00:00:00Z`. So
the site needs a **date-only** helper — parse the calendar date, do not shift it:

```ts
/** A calendar date - a day, not a moment. Not shifted by any timezone. */
export function parseApiDateOnly(value: string | null | undefined): string | null
```

and the locale formatting follows the existing `datetime.ts` pattern (the portal
already localises its dates — the new helper follows the same convention).

## 7. The three locales

`site/src/i18n/messages/{en,km,zh}/portal.json` — the portal already has the
`billing` and `payment` sections. The tax strings go there.

### 7.1 The keys

| Key | en | km | zh |
|---|---|---|---|
| `billing.tax.subtotal` | Subtotal | សមុទ្រថាន | 小计 |
| `billing.tax.label` | SST | SST | SST |
| `billing.tax.included` | incl. SST | រួមបញ្ចូល SST | 含 SST |
| `billing.tax.excluded` | + SST | + SST | + SST |
| `billing.tax.exempt` | Tax exempt | មិនទាន់កាត់ពន្ធ | 免税 |
| `billing.tax.servicePeriod` | Service period | រយៈពេលសេវា | 服务期间 |
| `billing.tax.registrationNumber` | SST registration no. | លេខចុះឈ្មោះ SST | SST 注册号 |
| `billing.tax.total` | Total | សរុប | 合计 |
| `payment.tax.breakdown` | Amount due | ប្រាក់ដែលត្រូវបង់ | 应付金额 |

### 7.2 The wording decisions

**`billing.tax.excluded` = "+ SST"** and **`included` = "incl. SST"**. The difference
is not cosmetic — `+` means *more to pay*, `incl.` means *already inside*. Getting
these the wrong way round is a pricing lie, so the wording is fixed in the copy, not
composed at runtime.

**The acronym "SST" stays "SST" in all three locales.** It is the term that appears on
the tax invoice and the term our accountant will use, and a translated term would not
match the document. In `km` and `zh` the **explanatory** strings are translated, so
the reader understands what the acronym is.

> **Open question for the accountant:** for a customer outside Malaysia, is the
> acronym right, or does the tax invoice have to carry the local tax name? If the
> Cambodian customer's invoice carries a different tax term, then `billing.tax.label`
> is **not a constant** — it becomes a field from the API, per customer. This is a
> one-line change later, but the copy should not hard-code the label in a way that
> assumes the answer.

### 7.3 The locales must not drift

Three locales, three keys each. If a key is missing from one file, that language falls
back to the key name — the customer sees `billing.tax.subtotal` on the screen. The
i18n check has to cover **all three** files, and the acceptance criteria say so.

## 8. The tax context — a new fetch in the portal

The portal needs to know, for the **signed-in customer**, whether a tax applies. That
is `GET /api/billing/tax-context`:

```json
{ "applicable": true, "treatment": "domestic_taxable", "rate": 0.08,
  "basis": "exclusive", "label": "SST", "registration_number": "W16-1234567-03" }
```

The portal reads it once, on the billing page and the invoice page. For a customer
under `export_pending` it returns `applicable: false` and the portal renders as it does
today.

The alternative — deriving the treatment client-side from the country — is **not**
acceptable. The treatment is a server-side determination with an effective date and a
ruling reference, and the client must not re-derive it.

---

## 9. What the customer must never see

This is the list that protects us from over-disclosure:

| Never shown | Why |
|---|---|
| `export_pending` | our uncertainty, not theirs |
| `export_exempt` as a headline | an exemption is our tax position, not their invoice |
| The threshold, the rate table, or the sources | internal |
| The net **without** the tax, when tax is charged | the gross is what they pay |
| A "SST 0.00" row | a zero row is noise; the row is absent |

---

## 10. Rollout in the site

| Step | What the customer sees |
|---|---|
| 0 | nothing — the site is byte-identical to today |
| 1 (shadow) | nothing — the tax is computed but not sent to the site |
| 2 (admin only) | nothing — the site still shows one number |
| 3 (display) | the tax line appears on the invoice; the portal and the checkout show the breakdown; **the amount charged is unchanged** |
| 4 (charge) | the gross includes the tax; the price pages carry the `+ SST` note |

Step 3 is where the customer first sees the tax — and it is deliberately **before**
the amount changes, so that the new number is never a surprise on the same day.

---

## 11. Acceptance criteria — site

- [ ] No customer-facing screen mentions `export_pending` or `export_exempt`.
- [ ] A taxable customer sees the net, the tax and the gross on the invoice, and the
      gross equals the amount that was charged.
- [ ] A non-taxable customer sees exactly the screen they see today — one number, no
      tax row.
- [ ] The service period renders as the correct calendar dates in **every** locale and
      in the reader's own timezone — a period ending 30 Nov never shows as 29 Nov.
- [ ] The tax line, the service period and the breakdown exist in **en**, **km** and
      **zh** — no missing key falls back to the raw key name.
- [ ] `+ SST` and `incl. SST` are never swapped: the `+` variant is used only for the
      exclusive basis.
- [ ] The checkout's displayed total equals the charged amount, to the cent.
- [ ] The price pages carry the tax note only once the tax is active, and the note
      matches the pricing basis in force.
- [ ] While the tax is not active, the site is unchanged — no note, no label, no
      empty row.
