## Priority 1: High Priority (Critical for F&B Operations)

### 1. Order Modifiers & Customization

**Description:** Allow customers to customize their orders with modifiers.

**Features:**
- Extra toppings / Remove ingredients
- Size options (Small, Medium, Large)
- Spice levels (Mild, Medium, Hot, Extra Hot)
- Special instructions per item
- Add-ons at additional cost

**Impact:**
- Essential for F&B businesses (cafes, restaurants, fast food)
- Increases average order value through upselling
- Reduces kitchen errors with clear modifications

**Implementation Effort:** Medium

---

### 2. Combo / Set Meals

**Description:** Bundle multiple items together at a discounted price.

**Features:**
- Create combo sets (e.g., Set A: Burger + Fries + Drink)
- Allow substitution of items within combo
- Different pricing tiers for combos
- Combo-specific modifiers

**Impact:**
- Increases average order value by 20-30%
- Drives faster checkout for repetitive orders
- Common requirement for QSR and fast-casual restaurants

**Implementation Effort:** Medium

---

### 3. Split Bill Enhancements

**Description:** Advanced bill splitting capabilities for group dining.

**Features:**
- Split by item (each item to different person)
- Split by seat / Person (equal items per person)
- Equal split among X persons
- Split by payment method (some cash, some card)
- Multiple payment methods in single transaction
- Track who paid what

**Current Status:** Partial implementation exists

**Impact:**
- Critical for fine dining and group bookings
- Reduces payment disputes
- Improves customer satisfaction

**Implementation Effort:** Low-Medium

---

## Priority 2: Medium Priority (Operational Efficiency)

### 4. Kitchen Routing / Station Labels

**Description:** Route order items to specific kitchen stations.

**Features:**
- Assign menu items to stations (Grill, Fryer, Salad, Bar, Dessert)
- Multiple printers for different stations
- Kitchen Display System (KDS) per station
- Fire course / Course sequencing
- Bump bar / kitchen bump functionality

**Impact:**
- Reduces ticket time
- Improves kitchen organization
- Essential for high-volume operations

**Implementation Effort:** Medium

---

### 5. Cash Drawer Management

**Description:** Track and reconcile cash transactions.

**Features:**
- Cash float setup at shift start
- Cash up / Cash reconciliation (X-Read, Z-Read)
- Track cash sales vs card vs e-wallet
- Cash drops during the day
- Over/short tracking
- Opening and closing cash counts

**Impact:**
- Critical for cash-heavy businesses
- Reduces shrinkage and theft
- Required for end-of-day closing

**Implementation Effort:** Low-Medium

---

### 6. Shift Reports

**Description:** Comprehensive shift-based reporting and closing.

**Features:**
- X-Read (current shift summary without reset)
- Z-Read (daily total with reset)
- Shift-wise breakdowns
- Staff clock-in/out report
- Cash reconciliation per shift
- Auto-close old shifts after configurable time

**Impact:**
- Essential for daily operations
- Enables accountability per shift
- Required for business reporting

**Implementation Effort:** Medium

---

### 7. Low Stock Alerts

**Description:** Automated notifications when inventory runs low.

**Features:**
- Configurable low stock threshold per ingredient
- Push notifications in-app
- Email alerts to managers
- Auto-suggest purchase orders when low
- Ingredient usage forecasting

**Impact:**
- Prevents out-of-stock situations
- Reduces kitchen rush for missing items
- Improves inventory management

**Implementation Effort:** Low-Medium

---

## Priority 3: Lower Priority (Growth Features)

### 8. Order Types

**Description:** Support different order fulfillment types.

**Features:**
- Dine In (with table assignment)
- Takeaway
- Delivery (with driver assignment)
- Drive-thru
- Self-pickup

**Impact:**
- Different workflows for each type
- Enables delivery integrations
- Future growth for delivery services

**Implementation Effort:** Medium

---

### 9. Waste & Comp Tracking

**Description:** Track wastage and complimentary items.

**Features:**
- Mark items as wasted (broken, expired, mistakes)
- Reason codes for wastage
- Complimentary item tracking
- Cost calculation for wastage
- Wastage reports

**Impact:**
- Identifies operational issues
- Helps reduce food costs
- Required for cost control

**Implementation Effort:** Low

---

### 10. Customer Display / Kiosk

**Description:** Customer-facing order confirmation and self-ordering.

**Features:**
- Order confirmation display (customer-facing screen)
- Self-ordering kiosk mode
- Menu browsing on customer display
- Order review before payment
- Integration with queue management

**Impact:**
- Reduces counter staff workload
- Enables 24/7 self-service
- Future expansion for quick-service restaurants

**Implementation Effort:** High

## Technical Considerations

### Database Schema Updates

Most features will require:
1. New tables for modifiers, combos, stations
2. Extension to order_items table for modifiers
3. New tracking tables for cash/logs/waste

### API Changes

- New endpoints for modifiers, combos, stations
- Extended order creation with order_type
- New reporting endpoints for shifts, cash

### Frontend Changes

- New UI components for modifiers modal
- Combo builder interface
- Kitchen routing display
- Cash drawer interface
