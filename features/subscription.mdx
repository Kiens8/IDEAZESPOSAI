# Subscription & Plans

Choose the plan that fits your restaurant's needs.

## Available Plans

| Plan | Price | Features |
|------|-------|----------|
| **Starter** | Free | Basic POS features |
| **Basic** | $10/month | + Full POS + Reports |
| **Pro** | $20/month | + Multi-outlet + Priority support |
| **Enterprise** | $50/month | + Advanced features + API access |

## Viewing Your Current Plan

1. Log in to your account
2. Go to **Billing** section
3. View your current plan and usage

## Upgrading Your Plan

1. Go to **Billing** → **Change Plan**
2. Select your desired plan
3. Choose payment method:
   - **Pay Now** - Stripe checkout
   - **Pay Later** - Invoice with 14-day payment terms

### Proration for Upgrades

When upgrading mid-cycle:

- **Example:** Basic ($10) → Pro ($20) with 15 days remaining
- **Credit:** $10 credit for unused Basic days
- **Charge:** $20 - $5 (credit) = $15 prorated amount

## Downgrading

Downgrade to a lower plan at any time. Changes take effect at the next billing cycle.

## Payment Methods

| Method | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Stripe** | Credit/Debit card via Stripe |
| **Pay Later** | Bank transfer (14-day terms) |

### Pay Later Flow

1. Select Pay Later at checkout
2. Receive invoice via email
3. Pay via bank transfer within 14 days
4. Invoice marked paid automatically

## Billing Portal

Access your billing portal to:

- View invoices
- Download receipts
- Update payment method
- Change plan
- Cancel subscription
